<p>The United States Army Corps of Engineers (USACE) Seattle District anticipates solicitation and award of a Firm Fixed-Price contract resulting from Request for Proposal (RFP) W912DW27RA014. The project intends to convert two (2) existing barrack buildings into an operational facility for 3 tier levels of command. These buildings in general are referred to as &ldquo;the Hammer Head&rdquo;. The Hammer Handle has three (3) floors with a crawl space at grade elevation. The Hammer Head is a single floor. The Basement area with a main utility mechanical &amp; electrical room extends partially under both the Hammer Head and the Hammer Handle. The definitive removal / demolition work items for these buildings will require: Hazardous material / debris abatement and removal mostly within the crawl space, partial at ~ 75% removal / demolition of exterior building envelopes, interior walls, etc, removal at ~ 90% removal of mechanical &ndash; electrical systems, removal of the existing roof EPDM system (but not the roof framing structure).</p>

<p></p>

<p>The definitive repair / new work items for these buildings will require new exterior envelope, new mechanical &ndash; electrical systems, new roof TPO system. Other considerations for site work coordination for a new utility connection (mostly by other entities) and shared construction boundaries with adjacent current and future projects.</p>
