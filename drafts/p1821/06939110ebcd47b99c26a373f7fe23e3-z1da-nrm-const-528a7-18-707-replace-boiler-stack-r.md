## Description
This pre-solicitation notice is to inform potential offerors that the Rome Community Based Outpatient Center (CBOC) in Rome, NY will be posting a solicitation (36C24227R0008) on or about October 30th, 2026, to provide all labor, materials, tools, equipment, and supervision necessary to complete the Replace Boiler Stack Rome CBOC project (528A7-18-707). 

This project is necessary to demolish two (2) existing boiler exhaust stacks and one (1) generator steam relief stack, the concrete / masonry chimney structure and seal the existing roof. The construction and installation of two (2) new steel boiler exhaust stacks, the steel support structure, and one (1) steel generator exhaust pipe at the Rome CBOC, 125 Brookley Road, Rome, NY 13441. 
It has been determined that there are qualified firms that can perform under NAICS Code 238220, "Plumbing, Heating, and Air Conditioning Contractors  with a size standard of $19M. This procurement is 100% set-aside for small businesses.  The estimated construction value of this project is between $500,000 and $1,000,000.  Information regarding the scheduled site visit will be stated in the Solicitation. Bid bonding will be required to respond to this RFP once released.
