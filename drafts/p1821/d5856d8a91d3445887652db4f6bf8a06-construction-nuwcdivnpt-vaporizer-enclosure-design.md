## Description
<p>The Naval Undersea Warfare Center Division, Newport (NUWCDIVNPT) has a construction requirement for the design/build of a vaporizer enclosure. &nbsp;Please see attached Solicitation (N6660426Q0292) and all attachments for full information and details regarding this requirement.</p>

<p>Contractors do not have to be JCP certified to receive the Solicitation, including drawings, specifications, and photos, but they must have an active registration in SAM.GOV.</p>

<p>Interested parties should request access to drawings, specifications, and photos within the attachments section of this solicitation. NUWCDIVNPT will review and approved requests within SAM.GOV.</p>

<p>A controlled attachment requires special handling due to the information it contains. You must have a data entry or administrator role within the Contract Opportunities domain for an entity in SAM.gov in order to request access. If you do not have a proper role, you can request it from an existing entity administrator or have the existing administrator assign roles directly to you. If your entity does not have a direct employee, officer or board member appointed as the Entity Administrator, you will need to complete an Entity Administrator Appointment Letter.</p>

<p>You must request access to the attachment by clicking on its title and providing the reason you need access.</p>

<p>You will not receive immediate access to view the document. The contracting officer will review your request to grant or reject your access.</p>

<p>Your entity is responsible for managing the documents in accordance with prescribed directives.</p>
