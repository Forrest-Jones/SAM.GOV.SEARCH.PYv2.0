## Description
<p>The U.S. Coast Guard Academy requires a qualified, licensed electrical contractor to inspect, repair as necessary, test, and restore Eaton/Cutler-Hammer MVS2 13.2 kV medium-voltage load interrupter switchgear, with primary work focused on Cabinet 5 and the associated 15 kV feeder cable serving Smith Hall. The contractor shall inspect Cabinet 5 for damage or missing/cannibalized components; remove and replace three Eaton 15CLE-250E fuses; procure and deliver three additional matching fuses for on-site spares; perform insulation-resistance testing of the feeder cable in accordance with current ANSI/NETA standards; provide certified test and field-service reports; coordinate all required outages and lockout/tagout procedures with the Government; and safely re-energize the cabinet and feeder upon successful completion of repairs and testing. All work shall comply with applicable OSHA, NFPA 70E, federal, state, and local requirements, including proper licensed personnel, arc-flash personal protective equipment, site protection, cleanup, and disposal of waste materials.</p>
