## Description
<p>The work includes replacement of secondary clarifier no. 4 internal components, replacement of control panels for all four secondary clarifiers. &nbsp;(PWO 24-0045)&nbsp;</p>

<p>The work at G575 includes phased construction of upgrades to manholes and piping(removal/replacement/new installation); removal and replacement of submersible pumps and removal and replacement of the existing crane over the wet well. (PWO 26-0044)</p>
