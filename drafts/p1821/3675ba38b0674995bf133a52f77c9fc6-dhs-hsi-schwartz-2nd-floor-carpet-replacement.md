- **Set-aside:** Small Business Set Aside - Total

## Description
<p>Contractor to provide all labor, materials and support necessary to replace approximately 6000 square feet of carpet and adjacent base boards in the HSI space on the 2nd floor of the Schwartz Federal Building, located at 880 Front Street, San Diego, CA 92101, in accordance with the statement of work. All furniture located in the space shall be relocated and moved back after carpet has been installed by the Contractor. The Contractor shall also be responsible for relocating any electrical and/or mechanical building system components necessary to perform this work.</p>
