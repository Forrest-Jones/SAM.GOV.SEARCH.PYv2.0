## Description
<p>THIS IS A REQUEST FOR INFORMATION (RFI) ONLY. 27 SOCONS is performing market surveillance to identify potential sources capable of providing vertical transportation equipment maintenance, repair, &amp; inspection services at Cannon AFB, NM. The Contractor shall provide all management, tools, supplies, equipment, parts, and labor necessary to maintain, repair, and inspect the real property Vertical Transportation Equipment (<strong>VTE</strong>), to <u>include elevators, vehicle lifts, and dock levelers</u> at Cannon Air Force Base, New Mexico in a manner that will ensure continuous and safe operation.</p>

<p>This is NOT a request for a quote, request for proposal, request for a prototype solution, or an invitation to bid. This is not to be construed as a commitment by the US Government to form a binding contract or agreement. The Government will not reimburse or pay for information submitted in response to this RFI.</p>
