## Description

<ol>
	<li>Title: Non-Destructive Testing Facility</li>
	<li>Location: Red River Army Depot, Texas</li>
</ol>

<p><strong>Scope: </strong>Construct an 8,000 SF Drive-through Depot Maintenance Shop for the non-destructive testing (NDT) of tactical vehicles, combat vehicles, and vehicle components. NDT is required to evaluate the integrity of welds, internal defects, structural capability, and condition of asset materials. The facility will include radiation protective shielding to allow for the existing x-ray equipment to be safely utilized at full potential and permit upgrades and improvements to the industrial x-ray system. The building will also facilitate the consolidation, expanded capability, as well as training for other NDT equipment including ultrasonic, dye penetrant, magnetic particle inspection, and laser scanning operations. The existing x-ray facility will be demolished.</p>

<p>The Target Price Range (TPR): The Government has established a&nbsp;TPR for construction&nbsp;estimated to be between $8,000,000 and $8,500,000. <u>The Construction Cost Limit (CCL) is estimated at $8,175,103.00&nbsp;</u>with a total Programmed Amount (PA) of $8,100,000.</p>
