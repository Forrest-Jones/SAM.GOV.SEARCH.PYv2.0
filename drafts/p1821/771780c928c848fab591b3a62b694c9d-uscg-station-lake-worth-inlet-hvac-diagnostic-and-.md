<p><strong>4. Scope of Work (Summary of Specifications)</strong></p>

<p><strong>4.1 General Work Requirements</strong></p>

<p>The Contractor shall furnish all necessary labor, materials, equipment, transportation, and supervision to perform a comprehensive diagnostic assessment and repairs of the facility&rsquo;s HVAC and cooling tower systems:</p>

<ol>
	<li><strong>Inspection &amp; Assessment:</strong> Inspect all HVAC equipment, cooling towers, circulating pumps, piping, controls, and associated electrical/mechanical distribution assemblies.</li>
	<li><strong>Operational Testing:</strong> Evaluate overall system condition, measure operational readings (pressures, temperatures, electrical draws, airflow), and identify functional deficiencies, component failures, and root causes of performance issues.</li>
	<li><strong>Corrective Actions &amp; Repairs:</strong> Execute required repairs, maintenance, component replacements, and necessary adjustments in accordance with manufacturer standards and National Electric Code (NEC).</li>
	<li><strong>Comprehensive Summary Report:</strong> Provide a formal engineering/technical diagnostic report including detailed findings, photographic documentation, recommended follow-on corrective actions, and budgetary cost estimations.</li>
</ol>

<p><strong>4.2 Contractor Qualifications &amp; Technical Standards</strong></p>

<ul>
	<li><strong>Licensing:</strong> All installation, diagnostic, and repair work shall be performed by an HVAC contractor actively licensed and certified in the State of Florida.</li>
	<li><strong>Code Compliance:</strong> All electrical and mechanical work shall strictly comply with the most current edition of the National Electrical Code (NEC) and applicable ASHRAE standards.</li>
	<li><strong>Safety &amp; Hot Work:</strong> The contractor shall comply with all Coast Guard safety regulations, including obtaining required hot work permits prior to open-flame brazing, cutting, or welding.</li>
	<li><strong>Utilities &amp; Facility Operations:</strong> Reasonable quantities of electricity and potable water will be made available without charge. The contractor shall furnish necessary backflow preventers, connections, and temporary facilities if required. Coordinate all staging and equipment laydown areas with the COR.</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>4.3 Required Submittals &amp; Deliverables</strong></p>

<ul>
	<li><strong>Personnel Roster:</strong> Full roster of on-site personnel submitted prior to pre-work conference (First Name, Last Name, Job Title, Company). All personnel must comply with the REAL ID Act of 2005 for base entry.</li>
	<li><strong>Safety Program Plan:</strong> Outlining accident prevention, fall protection, and site-specific hazard mitigation.</li>
	<li><strong>Project Schedule:</strong> Work progression schedule showing milestones within the 14-calendar-day performance period.</li>
	<li><strong>Product Data Sheets:</strong> Technical catalog cuts and manufacturer specifications for any replaced components.</li>
	<li><strong>Operation &amp; Maintenance (O&amp;M) Manuals &amp; Training:</strong> Electronic submittal containing warranty details, parts list, test data, startup/shutdown sequence, and provision of operational training to designated station personnel prior to final acceptance.</li>
</ul>
