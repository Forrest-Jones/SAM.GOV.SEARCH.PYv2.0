<p>The Department of Homeland Security, Federal Emergency Management Agency, Logistics Management Directorate, is conducting market research to identify contractors capable of providing on-site generator maintenance and testing services at four Continental United States locations (CONUS). The anticipated requirement may include key personnel, technician labor, specialized tooling, diagnostic instruments, vehicles, materials, consumables, transportation, preventive maintenance, corrective maintenance, testing, and related generator support services.&nbsp;</p>

<p>The anticipated locations are:&nbsp;</p>

<p>&bull;&nbsp;&nbsp; &nbsp;DC Atlanta, 3780 Southside Industrial Ct., Atlanta, GA 30354<br />
&bull;&nbsp;&nbsp; &nbsp;DC Fort Worth, 501 W. Felix, Fort Worth, TX 76115<br />
&bull;&nbsp;&nbsp; &nbsp;DC Greencastle, 12879 Molly Pitcher Hwy, Greencastle, PA 17225<br />
&bull;&nbsp;&nbsp; &nbsp;DC Tracy, 1547 East Grant Line Road, Tracy, CA 95304<br />
&nbsp;<br />
This RFI is issued solely for information gathering purposes to identify sources that can provide support for the LMD Continental United States Generator Maintenance (CONUS Generator Maintenance). In accordance with FAR 15.101(c), RFIs may be used when the Government wants to obtain price, delivery, other market information, and capabilities for planning purposes. Responses to these RFIs are not offers and cannot be accepted by the Government to form a binding contract. &nbsp;This RFI shall not be construed as a commitment by the Government for any purpose, nor does it restrict the Government to an ultimate acquisition approach. Respondents are solely responsible for all expenses associated with their RFI response. Respondents will not receive additional information in response to their submittal to the RFI and will not be notified of the resulting market research.</p>

<p>Respondents shall identify any proprietary information in their RFI response. Information submitted in response to this RFI will be used at the discretion of the Government. Further, the information submitted will remain confidential insofar as permitted by law, including the Freedom of Information and Privacy Acts. FEMA reserves the right to utilize any non-proprietary technical information in the anticipated PWS or solicitation. &nbsp;Although it is not necessary to address in their response, respondents shall be aware of any potential, actual, or perceived organizational conflicts of interest. Any real or potential conflicts must be sufficiently mitigated prior to a contract award. For further guidance, refer to the Federal Acquisition Regulation, Subpart 3.11<br />
