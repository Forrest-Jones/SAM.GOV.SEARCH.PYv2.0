VOLUME 1   FACTOR 1: TECHNICAL CAPABILITY

Technical Capability: Technical capability is defined as the vendor s ability to satisfy the solicitation s requirements, providing the highest quality service to VA New Jersey Health Care System (VANJHCS) facilities.  Vendor shall provide a maximum 15-page capability statement demonstrating their ability to meet all the requirements of the Statement of Work (SOW).  Vendor shall provide a petroleum bulk storage tank maintenance plan.
Understanding of the Requirement: Vendor shall submit a proposal that demonstrates its understanding of the requirement in accordance with the Statement of Work (SOW) and the extent to which potential risks are identified and mitigated. A copy and paste of the SOW is not sufficient. The vendor must demonstrate that it is a contractor regularly engaged in the field of Petroleum Bulk Storage Tank and Responding to Emergency Situations, such as oil spills. Being dependent upon an ostensible subcontractor will be considered as a high-risk factor in evaluation. 
Contractor Experience: The vendor shall provide 3-5 instances of past experiences performing work similar in nature, scope, complexity, and size to that which will be supplied under the prospective contract contemplated by this solicitation. If the vendor does not have work of this complexity and size, then the vendor shall provide up to 3 instances of experience of lesser complexity or magnitude to that required. Having no experience with work of similar nature will be considered a high-risk factor in evaluation. 
Licensing/Certifications: Vendor shall provide proof of its NJ License and Certification:  Vendor shall provide proof that all employees under the contract, including service technicians, and drivers/transporters under this requirement are licensed or certified to handle petroleum bulk storage tank under all Federal {U.S. Environmental Protection Agency (EPA); US Occupational Safety and Health Agency (OSHA)}, State {New Jersey Department of Environmental Protection (DEP)} laws, policies, regulations, and guidelines. Offeror shall provide proof that the offeror is licensed to conduct business as NJDEP UST Certification in New Jersey. At least one of the vendor s employees must have completed the NJDEP UST Training for Class A/B Operators (Rutgers University) and passed the ICC exam.  If the offeror does not possess these licenses/certifications themselves and rely heavily upon an ostensible subcontractor to hold the licenses/certificates this will be considered a high-risk factor in evaluation.
