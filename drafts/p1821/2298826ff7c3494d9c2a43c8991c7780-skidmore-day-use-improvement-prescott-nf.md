## Description
<p>This is a combined synopsis/solicitation for commercial items prepared in accordance with the format in FAR Part 12. This announcement constitutes the only solicitation. Quotations are being requested and a separate written solicitation will not be issued.</p>

<p>Solicitation number 127EAX26Q0050 is issued as a Request for Quotation (RFQ) for construction of new parking areas, entrances, barriers, and amenities for the Skidmore Day Use Area. Option items include additional steel barriers, new ramadas, and double-vault toilet.</p>

<p>This acquisition is set-aside for small business concerns. The applicable North American Industry Classification Standard Code is 238990, All Other Specialty Trade Contractors. The small business size standard is $16.50M. This acquisition is a Total Small Business Set-Aside. All responsible sources may submit a quotation which will be considered by the agency.</p>
