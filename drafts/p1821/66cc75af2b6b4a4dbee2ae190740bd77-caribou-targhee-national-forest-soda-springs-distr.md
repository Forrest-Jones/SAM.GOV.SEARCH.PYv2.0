## Description
<p>This project includes demolition of 12 mod dampers and associated thermostats, demolition of control modules in furnace room and connecting one new programmable thermostat direct to each of three furnaces. Rebalancing all diffusers.</p>

<p>See attached documents for Statement of Work and Specifications. Please note the period of performance has been extended through March 31, 2027.</p>

<p>Questions regarding this project are due no later than Monday September 21, 2026. Send your questions to Cassandra Potts via email at cassandra.potts@usda.gov</p>
