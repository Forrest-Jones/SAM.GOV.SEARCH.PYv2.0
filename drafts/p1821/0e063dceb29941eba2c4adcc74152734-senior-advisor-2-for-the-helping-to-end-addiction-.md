## Description
<p><strong>COMBINED SYNOPSIS / SOLICITATION (COMPETITIVE)</strong></p>

<p><strong>Title: Senior Advisor 2 for the Helping to End Addiction Long-term Initiative, or NIH HEAL</strong></p>

<p><strong>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; &nbsp;Initiative to act as Subject Matter Expert (SME) with Pharmaceutical Development and</strong></p>

<p><strong>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; &nbsp;&nbsp;Pain Expertise.</strong></p>

<p>&nbsp;(i)&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; This is a combined synopsis/solicitation for commercial products or commercial services prepared in accordance with RFO Part 12. This announcement constitutes the only solicitation. Offers are being requested and a separate written solicitation will not be issued.</p>

<p>The solicitation number is 7571PSQ00012 issued as a Request for Quote for a Senior Advisor 2 for the Helping to End Addiction Long-term Initiative.</p>

<p>The Government reserves the right to make one award, multiple awards, or no awards as a result of this solicitation.</p>

<p>This acquisition<em>&nbsp;is&nbsp;</em>set-aside for small business concerns. This solicitation incorporates provisions and clauses by reference. The full text of provisions and clauses may be accessed electronically at&nbsp;<em>www.acquisition.gov</em>.</p>

<p>The Defense Priorities and Allocations System (DPAS) are not applicable to this requirement.</p>

<p>(ii)&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; This requirement is for the following services:</p>

<p>NINDS is seeking to procure a highly experienced executive-level, drug discovery and development Senior Advisor 2 with extensive expertise in drug discovery and development, in support of the NIH HEAL Initiative, an aggressive, trans-agency effort to speed scientific solutions to stem the national opioid public health crisis.</p>

<p>(iii)&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; Description of requirement:<em> </em></p>

<p>The NIH is seeking to procure a highly experienced <strong><em><u>executive-level Senior Advisor 2</u></em></strong>, drug discovery and development subject matter expert with experience in the area of Pharmacology and Pain to provide feedback to the NIH program leadership and technical guidance on drug discovery and development projects within the NINDS HEAL portfolio. The Contractor will serve as a member of the HEAL External Oversight Committee, which will provide strategic guidance on HEAL projects and on occasion other NIH drug discovery and development programs.</p>

<p>See Attachment #1 - Statement of Work (SOW) for more information.</p>

<p><strong><u>Business Instructions:</u></strong></p>

<p>Quoters are required to complete Attachment 4 &ndash; Price Template. Quoters are only to fill in the highlighted cells (including the proposed labor rate and the number of consultants being proposed); all other cells will then auto-populate. Quoters must submit the completed pricing template in Excel (do not convert to PDF) as a separate attachment and shall not combine or embed the pricing template with other materials.</p>

<p>Quoters will note that the Government anticipates estimated 50 labor hours per year, with an option for up-to-additional 50 hours per year. It should also be noted that the Government may reimburse the successful Quoter(s) for up to $2,000 in annual travel-related expenses, per-consultant, that are necessary for performance of this requirement. These funds shall be provided only after it is determined there is a need for travel. Additionally, all travel requests require advance approval by the Government.</p>

<p>If the Quoter proposes the involvement of any additional staff besides the Key Personnel, it must clearly articulate their role and why it believes additional personnel are necessary to fulfill the requirement. The Quoter must also specify how this time will be charged (e.g. direct hourly rate or included within overhead). Finally, the Quoter is required to document its rationale for any proposed Other Direct Costs (ODCs) besides travel as well as the total amount. If any of these conditions are applicable, the Quoter shall add rows for the additional costs to the Price Template and include a Price Narrative in its response.</p>

<p>Salary Rate Limitation:</p>

<p>Quoters are advised that no NIH funds may be used to pay the direct annual salary of an individual through any contract awarded as a result of this solicitation at a rate in excess of the Executive Schedule, Level II* (direct salary is exclusive of Overhead, Fringe Benefits and General and Administrative expenses, also referred to as &quot;indirect cost&quot; or &quot;facilities and administrative (F&amp;A) costs&quot;). Direct salary has the same meaning as the term &quot;institutional base salary.&quot; An individual&#39;s direct salary (or institutional base salary) is the annual compensation that the Quoter pays for an individual&#39;s appointment whether that individual&#39;s time is spent on research, teaching, patient care or other activities. Direct salary (or institutional base salary) excludes any income that an individual may be permitted to earn outside of duties to the Quoter.</p>

<p>This does not preclude Quoters from absorbing that portion of an employee&#39;s annual salary (plus the dollar amount for fringe benefits and associated indirect costs) that exceeds a rate of the Executive Schedule, Level II*. The Executive Schedule, Level II* annual salary rate limitation also applies to individuals proposed under subcontracts as well as consultants.</p>
