## Description
<p>(i) This is a combined synopsis/solicitation for commercial products or commercial services prepared in accordance with part 12. This announcement constitutes the only solicitation. Offers are being requested and a separate written solicitation will not be issued. All quotes provided shall be marked &ldquo;Quote current until 30 September 2026.&rdquo;</p>

<p>This solicitation, W50S7T-26-Q-A013 is being issued as a Request for Quotation (RFQ).</p>

<p>This acquisition is set-aside for small business concerns. This solicitation incorporates provisions and clauses by reference. The full text of provisions and clauses may be accessed electronically at www.acquisition.gov.</p>

<p>This solicitation is 100% set-aside for small business under the North American Industry Classification System (NAICS) code 811310, with a small business size standard of $12,500,000.00. Product Service Code (PSC) is J039. The contracting officer reserves the right to award under a different NAICS if the company would qualify as a small business under a different NAICS.</p>

<p>The Government intends to award one (1) Purchase Order, it will shall be awarded with a one (1) year period of performance from 23 September 2026 to 22 September 2027 with four (4) option years available for extension.</p>

<p></p>

<p>(ii) Requirement Line Items:</p>

<ol>
	<li>Line Item 0001 - Perform certified annual inspections and routine preventive maintenance on eleven (11) Aerial Work Platforms (AWPs) assigned to the 182nd Airlift Wing Maintenance Group (182 AW/MXG), Base Year, Unit of Issue</li>
	<li>Line Item 1001 - Perform certified annual inspections and routine preventive maintenance on eleven (11) Aerial Work Platforms (AWPs) assigned to the 182nd Airlift Wing Maintenance Group (182 AW/MXG), Option Year 1, Unit of Issue</li>
	<li>Line Item 2001 &ndash; Perform certified annual inspections and routine preventive maintenance on eleven (11) Aerial Work Platforms (AWPs) assigned to the 182nd Airlift Wing Maintenance Group (182 AW/MXG), Option Year 2, Unit of Issue</li>
	<li>Line Item 3001- Perform certified annual inspections and routine preventive maintenance on eleven (11) Aerial Work Platforms (AWPs) assigned to the 182nd Airlift Wing Maintenance Group (182 AW/MXG), Option Year 3, Unit of Issue</li>
	<li>Line Item 4001 - Perform certified annual inspections and routine preventive maintenance on eleven (11) Aerial Work Platforms (AWPs) assigned to the 182nd Airlift Wing Maintenance Group (182 AW/MXG), Option Year 4, Unit of Issue</li>
</ol>

<p>(iii) Description of Requirement:</p>

<p>This is a non-personal services contract to provide all management, tools, supplies, equipment, transportation, certified labor, and supervision necessary to perform certified annual safety inspections and routine preventive maintenance (PM) services on a fleet of eleven (11) Aerial Work Platforms (AWPs) assigned to the 182nd Airlift Wing Maintenance Group (182 AW/MXG). All services shall be performed in accordance with manufacturer technical specifications, federal safety mandates, and the requirements detailed in this Statement of Work (SOW).</p>

<p>(iv) Dates and Location: The period of performance will be 23 September 2026 through 22 September 2027. The locations will be Peoria Air National Guard Base, Illinois.</p>
