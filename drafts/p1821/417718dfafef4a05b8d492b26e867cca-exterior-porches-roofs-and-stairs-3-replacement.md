## Description
<p>This requirement is for the demolition of three (3) existing exterior metal stair sets with porch roofs and the design, fabrication and installation of replacement sets for the US Naval Research Laboratory (NRL), Bldg. A59. The full description and response requirements are included in the Request for Quotes (RFQ) N0017326Q1904 along with attachments.</p>

<p>A site visit is required before submitting any proposal or quote. The site visit is scheduled for Wednesday, September 9, at 10:00am EDT at NRL. All visitors must notify NRL Contracts at least 2 days prior to the scheduled visit. If you are unable to attend on this date, another date may be requested, although the requested date/time may not be available.&nbsp; No change to the response date and time specified above will be granted based on an inability to visit NRL on the planned site visit day. Your quick response is appreciated to facilitate an award prior to current fiscal year end. &nbsp;</p>

<p>Amendment N0017326Q19040001 provides photos to show existing structures and conditions.</p>
