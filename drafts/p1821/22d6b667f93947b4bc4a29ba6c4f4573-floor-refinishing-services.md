- **Eligibility:** eligible_set_aside

- **Set-aside:** Small Business Set Aside - Total
- **Place of performance:** New London, CT
- **Response deadline:** 2026-11-13
- **SAM.gov link:** https://sam.gov/workspace/contract/opp/22d6b667f93947b4bc4a29ba6c4f4573/view

## Description
<p>The U.S. Coast Guard Academy in New London, Connecticut, requires a qualified contractor to provide all labor, materials, equipment, supervision, transportation, and incidental services necessary to inspect, prepare, sand, refinish, cure, and clean Government-owned hardwood athletic basketball court flooring at Roland Hall Gymnasium, approximately 17,422 square feet and serviced annually, and Billard Hall Gymnasium, approximately 12,129 square feet and serviced bi-annually. Services shall include protection or reapplication of existing court markings as required, use of commercial-grade indoor athletic floor finishes, removal of Contractor-generated debris, compliance with applicable safety and facility requirements, and coordination with the Contracting Officer&rsquo;s Representative to minimize disruption to Academy operations.</p>
