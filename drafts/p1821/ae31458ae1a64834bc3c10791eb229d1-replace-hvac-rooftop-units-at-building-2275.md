## Description
<p>Project Information</p>

<p>This pre-solicitation notice is being issued in accordance with RFO FAR 12.201-1 and RFO FAR 5.1.&nbsp; The purpose of this synopsis is to gain knowledge of potential sources for this project including interested Small Business Concerns.&nbsp; Its sole purpose is to provide information for planning and market research purposes only and shall not be construed as either a solicitation or obligation on the part of the Government.&nbsp; The Government welcomes responses from all interested parties; however, no decision or award of contract will be made based on responses from industry. The Government will not pay for the preparation of, nor the subsequent use of, information submitted in response to this Solicitation. Respondents will not be notified of the outcome of the Government&rsquo;s review of the information received. Additionally, the Government does not intend to hold discussions concerning this Solicitation with any interested parties but reserves the right to contact vendors if additional information is required for clarifications as it pertains to the Solicitation.</p>

<p>The contractor shall provide all plant, labor, new and current material, transportation, tools, equipment, appliances, supplies, services, permits, insurance, and supervision necessary to replace HVAC Rooftop Units at Building 2275 for the 163 ATKW CA ANG at March ARB.</p>

<p>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; Replace 6 Package Heat Pumps on existing curbs.</p>

<ol>
	<li>&nbsp;Replace disconnect switches and conduit.</li>
	<li>Salvage Automated Logic Control boards and install in units. Demonstrate functionality at EMSC Workstation in Building 2276.</li>
	<li>Reconnect to Fire Alarm room smoke detection shut down and provide testing.&nbsp; *Test in present of Base Fire Department.</li>
	<li>Provide flashing as required.</li>
	<li>Provide 5 year full warranty parts and labor</li>
	<li>Provide Commissioning.</li>
	<li>Crane Lift Plan submittal to include LL, SE, AGL AMSL, OE/AAA, &amp; FAA 7460-1. *Submit ASN to FAA and obtain approval. Schedule three-month review period.</li>
</ol>

<p>In accordance with FAR Supplement 36.204, Disclosure of the Magnitude of Construction Projects, the anticipated magnitude of construction is between $100,000 and $250,000. The NAICS code for this requirement is 238220, Commercial and Institutional Building Construction. The Small Business Administration standard for small businesses in this industry is $19,000,000.</p>
