## Description
<p>The Federal Bureau of Prisons (FBOP), Federal Correctional Institution (FCI) Elkton, anticipates the issuance of a solicitation for the award of a firm-fixed-price construction contract for a project titled Upgrade Fire Pump for FCI Elkton located&nbsp;8730 Scroggs Road, Lisbon, OH 44432.&nbsp;</p>

<p><strong>Solicitation Number:</strong>&nbsp;15BBNF26Q21600002 and is issued as a Request for Quotation (RFQ)</p>

<p><strong><u>SCOPE OF WORK:</u></strong></p>

<p>The contractor shall provide all labor, materials, equipment, tools, and incidentals necessary to remove the existing diesel fire pump controller and associated components and furnish and install a new UL‑listed diesel fire pump controller in a NEMA 4/4X enclosure. Work includes demolition of obsolete battery racks, conduit, and wiring; installation of new underground conduit and communication lines; reconnection of engine control circuits, sensors, and dual‑battery systems; and integration with the facility&rsquo;s Siemens DDC and Desigo CC automation platforms. All work must be performed in compliance with NFPA 20, applicable Federal, State, and local codes, and Bureau of Prisons technical requirements. Commissioning will include hydrostatic testing, flow/no‑flow testing, controller functional checks, and alarm/signal verification. See the attached Statement of Work and Drawings for complete technical specifications.</p>

<p>The estimated magnitude of the project is between $250,000 and $500,000.</p>
