## Description
<p>The Contractor shall provide all necessary parts, labor, materials, tools, equipment, supervision, and engineering services to disconnect and dispose of old UPS; and hardwire install and connect a customer-supplied UPS in Building 950, Fort Eustis, VA. The installed system will provide backup power to critical mods and sims systems in the event of power loss or interruption.<br />
<br />
See attached RFQ Document and PWS for full scope of work.</p>
