## Description
<p>THIS IS A SOURCES SOUGHT NOTICE ONLY. THIS IS NOT A REQUEST FOR PROPOSAL, REQUEST FOR QUOTE, OR INVITATION FOR BID. THERE WILL NOT BE A SOLICITATION, SPECIFICATIONS OR DRAWINGS AVAILABLE AT THIS TIME.</p>

<p>The Naval Facilities Engineering Systems Command, Mid-Atlantic is seeking eligible small business firms capable of performing investigation and repair services as well as removal and disposal of all Zebra Mussels and marine deposits attached to the active Raw Water Intake (RWI) and associated timber crib structure at Naval Station Great Lakes, Great Lakes, IL.</p>

<p>The following are specialty qualifications necessary to perform this work:</p>

<ul>
	<li>The Project Manager shall supervise a qualified crew of divers throughout the duration of this contract.</li>
	<li>Boat operators shall have completed a boaters safety course and have a valid Boating Safety Certificate.</li>
	<li>Divers shall have a minimum of one-year commercial diving experience in the applicable position; divers shall have completed at least four (4) working dives with similar decompression techniques, diving techniques, and equipment utilized under this contract. Divers shall demonstrate that at least one (1) of the four (4) qualified dives were performed in the last six (6) months prior to the contract award date. Each dive team member shall have a current certification in CPR, first aid, use of emergency oxygen systems, and all other applicable qualifications listed in the OSHA 29 CFR 1910.410.</li>
</ul>

<p><strong><u>General Work Requirements:</u></strong></p>

<p></p>

<p>Except where otherwise stated, the Contractor shall furnish all labor, tools, transportation, supervision, materials, inspections, equipment, and necessary safety compliance to perform all work required, to investigate and repair the existing raw water intake and provide Zebra Mussel and marine deposit removal and disposal at the Naval Station Great Lakes, Great Lakes, IL. The work may include penetration diving inside the raw water intake piping to inspect or repair elements of the intake system or to remove marine deposits. The work shall be performed at the Naval Station Water Treatment Plant.</p>

<p>This is a new procurement. This does replace an existing contract. The anticipated award of this contract is June 2027.</p>

<p>The North American Industry Classification System (NAICS) Code for this project is 238990, All Other Specialty Trade Contractors, with a small business size standard of $19 million.</p>
