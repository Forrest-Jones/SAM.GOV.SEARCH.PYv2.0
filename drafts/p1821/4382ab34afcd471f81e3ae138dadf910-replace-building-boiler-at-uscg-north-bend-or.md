## Description
<p><strong>DESCRIPTION OF REQUIREMENT:&nbsp;</strong></p>

<p>The U.S. Coast Guard requires services for a contractor to provide all personnel, equipment, tools, materials, transportation, supervision, and other items and services necessary to remove and replace the existing hydronic boiler, including associated components, control programming and integration, commissioning, and onsite system operating training- located in the McAdams Building at US Coast Guard BASE North Bend. The Government intends to issue a&nbsp;<strong>firm fixed price</strong>&nbsp;contract for this requirement. The full scope of work and specifications will be included in the solicitation documents.</p>

<p>North Bend, OR 97459</p>

<p>This is a combined synopsis/solicitation for commercial products or commercial services prepared in accordance with part 12. This announcement constitutes the only solicitation. Offers are being requested and a separate written solicitation will not be issued.</p>

<p>Solicitation number 70Z03326QSEAT0120 is issued as a&nbsp;<em>RFQ</em>&nbsp;for&nbsp;removal and replacement of boiler.</p>
