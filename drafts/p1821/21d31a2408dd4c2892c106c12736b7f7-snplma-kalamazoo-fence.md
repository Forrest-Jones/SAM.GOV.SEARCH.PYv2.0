- **Response deadline:** 2026-12-04

## Description
<p>This intent of this project is to construct approximately 0.5 miles of<br />
barbwire fence lines near Kalamazoo Campground on the Ely Ranger District. Construct two<br />
barbwire drift fences totaling approximately 0.5 miles in length to exclude active grazing within<br />
Kalamazoo Campground. Livestock in the campground presents a safety hazard and has resulted in<br />
numerous complaints from the public. In addition, limiting livestock activities within the<br />
campground will also extend protection of newly installed recreational infrastructure. This project<br />
includes construction of approximately 2,500 feet of new barbwire fence including installation of an<br />
estimated four 12-foot wire gates as a lump sum.</p>
