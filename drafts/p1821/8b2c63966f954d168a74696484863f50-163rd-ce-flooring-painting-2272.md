## Description
<p><strong>Project Information</strong></p>

<p>This is a Presolicitation Notice issued in accordance with RFO FAR 12.201-1 and RFO FAR 5.1.</p>

<p>NAICS Code: <strong>236220 - Commercial and Institutional Building Construction</strong></p>

<p>The California National Guard intends to issue a Request for Quotes (RFQ) to award a single firm fixed-price contract for services, non-personal, to provide all plant, labor, transportation, materials, tools, equipment, appliances and supervision necessary to <strong>replace flooring and paint</strong> interior walls at Building 2272 for the 163 ATKW CA Air National Guard (ANG) at <strong>March ARB, CA</strong>.</p>

<p><strong>Scope of Work:</strong></p>

<ol>
	<li>Demo VCT, carpet, vapor barrier, and rubber base.</li>
	<li>Prep floors and walls.</li>
	<li>Provide polished concrete (including submitting a test report for 50 GU @ 60 degrees).</li>
	<li>Provide vapor barrier and carpet tile.</li>
	<li>Provide rubber base.</li>
	<li>Paint interior walls.</li>
	<li>Work is specified and shown on the drawings.</li>
	<li>Provide a minimum of a one (1) year warranty on material and workmanship following the date of substantial completion.</li>
</ol>

<p>The contract duration will be <strong>60 calendar days</strong> after notice to proceed to include inspection and punch list. This project is intended to be set aside 100% for Small Businesses. The North American Industry Classification System (NAICS) Code is 236220, with a small business size standard of $45,000,000. The magnitude of construction is between $150,000 and $300,000.</p>

<p>Interested offerors must be registered in the System for Award Management (SAM). To register go to www.SAM.gov. Instructions for registering are on the web page (there is no fee for registration).</p>

<p>In accordance with RFO FAR 36.101-4, the following information is provided:</p>

<ol>
	<li>There are no agency-specific policies or procedures that apply to the definitization of equitable adjustments for change orders under construction contracts.</li>
	<li>No data is available regarding the timely definitization of equitable adjustments for change orders under construction contracts.</li>
</ol>

<p><strong>Instructions to Vendors</strong></p>

<p>All interested vendors will submit a written notice, not to exceed five (5) pages, stating their positive intention to submit a quote as a prime contractor no later than the deadline posted with this notice. Responses to this Solicitation should include the following information:</p>

<ol>
	<li>A positive statement of intent to submit a quote/proposal as a prime contractor.</li>
	<li>A listing of projects completed during the past five (5) years for both Government and private industry. The list should provide the type of project, dollar value, contract number, location, and point of contact. Also, please indicate whether the firm was a prime or subcontractor&mdash;if the firm was a subcontractor, please provide a point of contact with the prime contractor.</li>
	<li>A brief outline of resources, subcontractors, and key personnel that would be used to accomplish the contract, and Demonstration of the firm&rsquo;s socioeconomic status (i.e., SB, 8(a), HUBZone, EDWOSB, WOSB, SDVOSB, etc.), and size.</li>
</ol>

<p>The Government welcomes responses from all interested parties; however, no decision or award of contract will be made based on responses from industry. The Government will not pay for the preparation of, nor the subsequent use of, information submitted in response to this presolicitation notice. Respondents will not be notified of the outcome of the Government&rsquo;s review of the information received. Additionally, the Government does not intend to hold discussions concerning this Solicitation with any interested parties but reserves the right to contact vendors if additional information is required for clarifications as it pertains to the Solicitation.</p>

<p>If adequate small business firms are not available for adequate competition for this project, it may be advertised as an unrestricted solicitation. Submit responses directly to contacts below; telephone calls and facsimile responses will not be honored. Please refer to the Notice ID and project title in the subject of your message. Please limit your responses to standard documents (i.e., Microsoft Office, Adobe PDF, etc.). Do not send compressed (i.e., .zip or .exe extension) or other file types as they will be stripped from your message.</p>
