## Description
<p><strong>Offers</strong> are due <strong>25 SEPT</strong>. The U.S. Army Corps of Engineers Headquarters has a requirement to execute the full start-up and commissioning of existing data center environmental control systems based on original design specifications, as well as the replacement and EPA-compliant disposal of batteries for an Eaton Uninterruptible Power Supply (UPS) system.This procurement will be awarded under Simplified Acquisition Procedures. There will be one <strong>site visit a</strong>t the Cude Building 2592 on <strong>Friday, 18 September. </strong>The site visit is&nbsp;<strong>required.</strong>&nbsp; All contractors will need to meet at the CUDE Building 2592, 7701 Telegraph Road, Alexandria, Virginia 22315 for check-in beginning at 9am EST. All contractors who wish to be at the site visit must send their <strong>names, phone numbers, and company names </strong>to the Contract Specialist, Rick Klein, via email. All contractors must be US Citizen or US National and possess a valid Government ID. Must have driver license to enter Facilities. It is the Offeror&rsquo;s responsibility to ensure the quote has been received by the contracting office via email. Fax quotes will not be accepted. Late quotes will be processed in accordance with RFO 52.212-1(c) &ldquo;Late submission, modifications, revisions, and withdrawals of offers.&rdquo; Upon receipt of the electronic offer, the Government will acknowledge receipt of that offer via email to the Offeror.</p>
