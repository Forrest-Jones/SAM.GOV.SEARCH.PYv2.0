## Description
<p><strong>DESCRIPTION OF REQUIREMENT:&nbsp;</strong></p>

<p>The U.S. Coast Guard requires annual inspection, testing, and maintenance of the fire protection systems for multiple facilities located at USCG AIRSTA Port Angeles. The work consists of providing all labor, equipment, material, transportation, supervision, items and services necessary for properly performing inspection, testing, and maintenance of fire protection systems in multiple buildings, including an aircraft hangar, in accordance with NFPA 11, NFPA 25, NFPA 70, NFPA 72, and NFPA 80 at Air Station Port Angeles. The Government intends to issue a firm-fixed-price purchase order for this project. The full scope of work and specifications are included in the solicitation documents.</p>
