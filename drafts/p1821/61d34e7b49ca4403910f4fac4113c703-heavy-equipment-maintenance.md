## Description
<p>SOURCES SOUGHT</p>

<p>Heavy Equipment Maintenance &amp; Inspections &ndash; PEACE VANGUARD</p>

<p><strong>NOTE: THIS IS NOT A SOLICITATION FOR PROPOSALS. THIS IS A SOURCES SOUGHT NOTICE ONLY.</strong> This Sources Sought is for planning and informational purposes only and shall NOT be considered as a Request for Proposal (RFP) or as an obligation on the part of the Government to acquire any products or services. The Government will NOT be responsible for any costs incurred in responding to this Sources Sought or furnishing the requested information. No contract will be awarded from this announcement. Availability of any formal solicitation will be announced separately. Submittals will not be returned to the sender. Respondents will not be notified of the results of the Government&rsquo;s review.</p>

<p>1.0 Introduction</p>

<p>The National Guard Bureau&rsquo;s Operational Contracting Division Contracting Office (NGB-AQ-C), 111 South George Mason, Arlington, VA 22204 is issuing this Sources Sought as a means of conducting Market Research to determine the availability of capabilities to perform Light to Heavy Equipment Maintenance and Inspections for the United States Air Force Training Detachment (USAFTD) Peace Vanguard in Marana, AZ. Telephone inquiries will not be accepted or acknowledged, and no feedback or evaluations will be provided to companies regarding their submissions.</p>

<p>2.0 Description of Requirement</p>

<p>The contractor shall furnish all labor, supervision, transportation, equipment, materials, parts, and incidental services necessary to perform comprehensive maintenance, repair, modification, and safety certification services. The service supports aviation ground handling operations, fleet maintenance, personnel/cargo transit, and aircraft maintenance safety for the Republic of Singapore Air Force (RSAF) AH-64 Apache helicopter flight training operations.</p>

<p>The scope covers an estimated 150 pieces of equipment across the following categories:</p>

<ul>
	<li>
	<p><strong>Robin Air ECS Carts:</strong> Refrigerant recovery, recycling, evacuation, and recharging units.</p>
	</li>
	<li>
	<p><strong>Helicopter Heavy-Duty Diesel Industrial Tow Tractors:</strong> All-wheel-drive high-drawbar-pull diesel towing units.</p>
	</li>
	<li>
	<p><strong>Diesel Light Towers:</strong> Portable, trailer-mounted diesel-powered industrial floodlight towers.</p>
	</li>
	<li>
	<p><strong>Aircraft Tripod Jacks:</strong> Multi-stage vertical hydraulic stabilizers utilized for full fuselage lifting.</p>
	</li>
	<li>
	<p><strong>Aircraft Box (Axle) Jacks:</strong> Low-profile hydraulic systems used for landing gear, wheel, and brake maintenance.</p>
	</li>
	<li>
	<p><strong>Kawasaki Diesel Mules:</strong> Heavy-duty diesel utility task vehicles (UTVs).</p>
	</li>
	<li>
	<p><strong>Mobile Platform Stands:</strong> Height-adjustable, rolling structural maintenance stands.</p>
	</li>
	<li>
	<p><strong>Enclosed &amp; Open Utility Trailers:</strong> General-purpose cargo and equipment transport trailers.</p>
	</li>
	<li>
	<p><strong>Overhead Hoist Crane:</strong> Service, inspect, and load test overhead crane in the maintenance aircraft bay.</p>
	</li>
	<li>
	<p><strong>33x Sling Types:</strong> Certify and load test slings (wire rope, puller type, hoisting pins, lifting eyes, hooks).</p>
	</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>Logistical &amp; Performance Constraints:</strong></p>

<ul>
	<li>
	<p>Services are typically performed on-site via mobile service teams, or at the contractor&rsquo;s authorized facility for depot-level rebuilds.</p>
	</li>
	<li>
	<p>The Government will <strong>NOT</strong> provide lift equipment (e.g., forklifts); the contractor must arrive fully equipped..</p>
	</li>
	<li>
	<p>Mandatory annual load testing must be performed at 105% to 110% capacity for all tripod and box jacks.</p>
	</li>
	<li>
	<p>Technicians servicing Robin Air ECS carts must hold appropriate EPA Section 609 certifications.</p>
	</li>
</ul>

<p>The estimated period of performance shall be from <strong>31&nbsp;October 2026 to 31 December 2028 (26 months)</strong>. The contract type is anticipated to be Firm-Fixed Price.</p>
