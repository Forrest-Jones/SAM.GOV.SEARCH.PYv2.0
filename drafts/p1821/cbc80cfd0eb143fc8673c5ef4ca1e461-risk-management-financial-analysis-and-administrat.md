## Description
<p>COMBINED SYNOPSIS AND SOLICITATION REQUEST FOR PROPOSAL (RFP) 47QACA26R0306:</p>

<p>This is a combined synopsis/solicitation for commercial products or commercial services prepared<br />
in accordance with part 12. This announcement constitutes the only solicitation. Offers are being&nbsp;<br />
requested and a separate written solicitation will not be issued.&nbsp;</p>

<p>Solicitation number 47QACA26R0306 is issued as a request for proposal (RFP), for The Small&nbsp;<br />
Business Administration (SBA), the Office of Capital Access (OCA), Office of Credit Risk&nbsp;<br />
Management (OCRM), is seeking for a single-award indefinite delivery, indefinite quantity&nbsp;<br />
(IDIQ) contract to obtain non-personal services. The contract is for a base year and four option&nbsp;<br />
years. Task Orders will be issued on a labor-hours (LH) basis using the Contractor&rsquo;s fully loaded&nbsp;<br />
labor-hour rates, applicable labor categories, and required level of effort.</p>

<p>This is a 100% set-aside restricted to responsible SBA-certified Women-Owned Small Businesses<br />
&nbsp;(WOSB) participating in the WOSB Federal Contract Program. Offerors must be currently certified&nbsp;<br />
as WOSBs in the SBA WOSB certification system at the time of proposal submission and at the&nbsp;<br />
time of award.</p>

<p>(ii) Please see Attachment A SF 1449 and Attachment B pricing sheet of the solicitation for the list of contract line item(s) and estimated quantities (including option(s) for this requirement).</p>

<p>(iii) The Contractor shall provide personnel with the ability to augment the SBA&#39;s workforce and support OCRM at its Headquarters office in Washington, D.C. Personnel provided shall support OCRM&#39;s activities, to include but not limited to:<br />
Delegated Authority Management: Collection, inspection, analysis for delegated authority renewals, nominations, add-ons and re-delegations<br />
-Risk Diagnostics: Execution of Desktop Reviews, comprehensive financial analysis and diagnostic activities to assess 7(a) Lender risk.&nbsp;</p>

<p>Operational Systems Proficiency: Utilization of federal systems, specifically the Lender Portal and the Capital Access Financial System (CAFS).&nbsp;</p>

<p>Preparation of Internal Correspondence: Preparation of data, reports, and correspondence for internal and external stakeholders.</p>

<p>&nbsp;Provide assistance with 7(a) loan transfers, invoice resolution, and maintenance of risk identification protocols generation of deliverables to identify SBA Lenders that are, or should be, subject to increased supervision and/or enforcement actions<br />
(iv) The anticipated period of performance is October 6, 2026 through October 5, 2027 (not including the four (4) one-year option periods). &nbsp;The Risk Management Support Services will be provided at the location:<br />
Small Business Administration Headquarters<br />
409 3rd St. SW<br />
Washington, DC 20416</p>

<p>(v) The solicitation and incorporated provisions are those in effect the date of posting and are provided in Section I and K.
