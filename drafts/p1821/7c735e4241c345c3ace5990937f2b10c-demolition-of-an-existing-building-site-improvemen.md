## Description
The U.S. Army Corps of Engineers, New York District is issuing an invitation for bid to award a Firm Fixed Price construction contract for the demolition of existing building, site Improvements  and provide a turnkey modular office facility. The scope includes the demolition of an existing 10,000 SF building (with basement, in-ground pool, and pool shed), Hazardous Materials Abatement and Disposal, Site Improvements (SWPPP Controls, Sidewalks, Parking, Landscaping, Temp Fencing, Utilities Infrastructure  including Water, Sewer, Electrical, Telecom tie-ins), and construction of a new office space of approximately 5,700 square feet, composed of custom modular units. The facility will be a single-story complex with a u-shaped space opening onto a small deck. It will house open offices with cubicles, six private offices, two conference rooms, four single-user bathrooms, a kitchenette/pantry room, lactation room, storage closets, and a mechanical room. The project scope encompasses incidental demolition, site prep work, foundations, underground utility hook ups, and site lighting. The contractor will also be responsible for providing furniture, fixtures, and equipment based upon the drawings and specifications. The building and site infrastructure are required to have a minimum life expectancy of 10 to 15 years. The contract duration will be 136 calendar days for the entire project with phasing work involved. NAICS: 236220 Size standard: $45 million. Project magnitude is between $1,000,000 - $5,000,000.
