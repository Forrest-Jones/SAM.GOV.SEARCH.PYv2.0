## Description
<p>Combined Synopsis/Solicitation</p>

<p>Defense Logistics Agency (DLA) B34 and B42 DWV System Smoke Testing &amp; Hydro Jetting</p>

<p>In accordance with executive order 14721 which requires agencies to procure commercially available products and services to the maximum extent practicable, this is a solicitation for commercially available services prepared in accordance with the format in FAR Part 12, and supplemented with additional information included in this notice &nbsp;The solicitation number SP4703-27-Q-0003 is issued as a request for quotes (RFQ). &nbsp;The resulting contract will be a firm-fixed price award with an expected Period of Performance of 60 days from the issuance of the Notice to Proceed. This acquisition is set-aside for 100% small businesses. &nbsp;The North American Industry Classification System (NAICS) Code is 236220 (Office Buildings). &nbsp;The small business size standard is $45M. &nbsp;Department of Labor Wage Determination based on location of the service provided (Chesterfield County, VA). &nbsp;Partial proposals will not be accepted. &nbsp;</p>

<p>For an offer to be considered for award, the contractor must own, possess, or otherwise control a bona fide place of business from which mobilization occurs within 120 miles of DSCR. A bona fide place of business refers to a location where the offeror regularly maintains an office with at least one full-time employee. The offeror must include the address of this location in its bid.</p>

<p>OBJECTIVES: The Contractor shall provide all labor, supervision, materials, equipment, and incidentals necessary to perform smoke testing, hydro jetting and evaluations of the drain, waste and vent system.</p>

<p>BACKGROUND: Buildings 34 and 42 has experienced an intermittent sewer odor throughout the facilities. The Government requires a comprehensive evaluation of the Drain, Waste, and Vent system to identify potential defects, leaks, obstructions, improper connections, and other deficiencies contributing to the odor. No repairs are authorized under this contract. The purpose of this effort is to document the existing condition of the entire DWV system and provide recommendations for future corrective actions.</p>

<p>SPECIFIC REQUIREMENTS: Perform a complete smoke test of the entire DWV systems.<br />
o The Contractor shall introduce a non-toxic, non-staining plumbing smoke, or another industry approved standard testing medium, into the DWV systems.<br />
o The smoke used shall be specifically manufactured and intended for plumbing system testing.<br />
o If an alternative testing method is proposed, the Contractor shall obtain written approval from the PM prior to use and demonstrate that the method provides an equivalent level of leak detection.<br />
o Identify and document all locations where smoke is observed.<br />
o The Contractor shall hydro jet the entire accessible sanitary drain systems, including horizontal mains, branch lines, building sewer and vertical stacks where practical.<br />
o The Contractor shall perform a CCTV inspection of the entire accessible DWV systems and provide a USB thumb drive with footage of the inspection.<br />
o Provide a comprehensive written inspection report and a summary of smoke test findings and a complete hydro jetting report.<br />
o No repairs shall be performed under this contract.<br />
o If conditions requiring repairs are identified, the Contractor shall document the deficiency and include the location and recommend corrective action in the final report.<br />
o Due to building occupancy, all work shall be performed after normal business hours.<br />
o Normal business hours are between 0600-1800, Monday-Friday.</p>
