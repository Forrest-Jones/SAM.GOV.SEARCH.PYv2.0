## Description
<p>This is a combined synopsis/solicitation for commercial items prepared in accordance with the format in FAR Part 12.&nbsp; This announcement constitutes the only solicitation.&nbsp; Quotations are being requested and a separate written solicitation will not be issued.&nbsp;</p>

<p>Solicitation number 127EAX26Q0104 is issued as a Request for Quotation (RFQ) for Bldg 13 Window Repair Replacement</p>

<p>IAW Attachment 1 - Statement of Work, The Honeybee Breeding, Genetics &amp; Physiology Research Laboratory, has a requirement of:</p>

<p>Contractor will remove and replace existing windows in Building 13 (Barn) with energy-efficient windows matching the style and appearance of those previously installed in Buildings 11 and 22. Work includes replacement of (8) 3-foot by 3-foot windows and (1) 2-foot by 2-foot window on the south elevation, as well as (2) 5-foot by 3-foot windows and (4) 3-foot by 3-foot windows on the north elevation. New windows will be installed plumb, level, weather-tight, and in accordance with manufacturer recommendations.</p>

<p>This acquisition&nbsp; is set-aside for small business concerns.&nbsp; The applicable North American Industry Classification Standard Code is 238350 &ndash; Finish Carpentry Contractors.&nbsp; The small business size standard is $19M.&nbsp; This acquisition is a Total Small Business Set-Aside.&nbsp; All responsible sources may submit a quotation which will be considered by the agency.</p>
