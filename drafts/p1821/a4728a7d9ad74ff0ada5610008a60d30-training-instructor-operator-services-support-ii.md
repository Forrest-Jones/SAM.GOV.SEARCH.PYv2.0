<p>The U.S. Army Contracting Command (ACC-APG) Orlando Contracting Division (OCD), on behalf of the Capability Program Executive Office Simulation, Training, Test &amp; Threat (CPE ST3), is officially announcing the soliciting proposals for the <strong>TRAINING INSTRUCTOR OPERATOR SERVICES SUPPORT II (TRIOSS II)</strong>. This requirement is to procure continuous, uninterrupted I/O support for the MSTCs as well as support for other miscellaneous courses and training events located at Army MSTCs throughout the CONUS and OCONUS.</p>

<p>The anticipated contract is for a single award Firm Fixed Price Mandatory Minimum Staffing Baseline (MMSB) contract which has a base period and four 1-year option periods.&nbsp; Detailed instructions, performance work statements, and submission guidelines are fully detailed within the attached solicitation W900KK-27-R-A003.&nbsp;</p>

<p><strong>Proposals are due 09 November 2026 at 09:00 EDT</strong>.&nbsp; Late submissions will not be considered for evaluation.</p>

<p><strong><em>All questions must be received no later than 15 October 2026 at 11:00 AM Eastern Standard Time (EST), see Section L for submission instructions.</em></strong></p>

<p></p>

<p>Notice of Intent (Requested): To assist the Government in administrative preparations and oral presentation scheduling, all interested Prime Contractors intending to submit a proposal are requested to notify the Government via email at usarmy.orlando.cpe-st3.mbx.trioss-ii@army.mil within one (1) week of this posting.</p>

<p>Note: This notification is voluntary and does not preclude the evaluation of any timely received proposal.</p>

<p></p>

<p>Oral Presentations: Will be scheduled for in-person presentations at ACC-APG Orlando Contracting Division in Orlando, Florida and will be coordinated with Offerors after all proposals have been received, logged and reviewed to be compliant.</p>
