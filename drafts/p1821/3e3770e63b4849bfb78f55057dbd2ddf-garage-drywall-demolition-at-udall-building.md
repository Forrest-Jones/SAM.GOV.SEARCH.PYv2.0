## Description
(i)	This Request for Quote (RFQ) for construction work located at the Stewart Lee Udall Building, HQ for the Department of the Interior, 1849 C Street, NW, Washington DC 20240.<br/><br/>(ii)	This requirement is a total small business set-aside under North American Industry Classification System (NAICS) Code 238910  Site Preparation Contractors with a business size standard of $19 million. Federal Supply Code (FSC) Z2AA  Repair or Alteration of Office Buildings. This solicitation is issued under Revolutionary FAR Overhaul (RFO) Part 12, Acquisition of Commercial Products and Commercial Services.  <br/><br/>(iii)	This is a combined synopsis/solicitation.  This announcement constitutes the only solicitation; quotes are being requested and a separate written solicitation will not be issued. The Government will award a firm fixed price contract from this solicitation to the responsible vendor whose quote, conforming to the solicitation, will be most advantageous to the Government, price and other factors considered. The Government intends to evaluate all timely quotes received from eligible vendors on a lowest price technically acceptable (LPTA) basis. The Government intends to evaluate quotations and award a contract without discussions with quoters. The Government reserves the right to conduct discussions if the Contracting Officer later determines them to be necessary.<br/><br/><br/>Point of Contact: The Contracting Officer shall serve as the focal point for inquiries from actual or prospective offerors. Address any questions or concerns to Contracting Officer, Nicholas Lesicki; nicholas_lewicki@ibc.doi.gov and contract specialist Haider Al Azzawi; halazzawi@ibc.doi.gov. All inquiries must be written, no phone inquiries will be answered. Questions are due by 12:00 p.m. EST on November 10, 2026. Late questions may not be answered.<br/><br/>Quotes are due November 19, 2026, at 2:00 PM Eastern Standard Time (EST). Quotes shall be submitted to Contracting Officer, Nicholas Lewicki; nicholas_lewicki@ibc.doi.gov and contract specialist Haider Al Azzawi; halazzawi@ibc.doi.gov.<br/><br/><br/><br/>A site-visit is scheduled for interested vendors on Friday November 6, 2026 at 11:00AM and Monday, November 9, 2026 at 2:00PM. Vendors attending the site-visit must send an email to nicholas_lewicki@ibc.doi.gov and halazzawi@ibc.doi.gov indicating their business, name of person(s), phone number and email address of the representative(s) attending the site-visit. All visitors must have a valid REAL ID to enter the building. The address for the site visit is 1849 C Street NW, Washington, DC, 20240, using the C Street entrance. Please wait at the security desk for a representative to get you.<br/>
