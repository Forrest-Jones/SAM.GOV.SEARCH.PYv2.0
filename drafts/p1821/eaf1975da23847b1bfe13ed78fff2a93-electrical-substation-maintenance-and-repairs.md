## Description
<p>THIS A PRE-SOLICITATION NOTICE. The Mission &amp; Installation Contracting Command (MICC)-Fort Buchanan has a Request for Quote (RFQ) No. W912C327QA001 for Electrical Substation Maintenance and Repairs. A Firm Fixed Price contract will be awarded for this requirement. The RFQ will be issued on or about 23 November 2026.&nbsp;</p>

<p>The RFQ will be issued as 100% set-aside small business on a competitive basis. All responsible sources are invited to submit a quote under this solicitation.</p>

<p>The North American Industry Classification System (NAICS) code applicable to this requirement is 238210, Electrical Contractors and Other Wiring Installation Contractors. The size standard is $19 Million.</p>

<p>MICC has a requirement for Electrical Substation Maintenance and Repairs, including all necessary administrative functions, personnel, materials, tools, equipment, services, transportation, and insurance required to perform the services outlined in the PWS. The requirement supports Fort Buchanan&rsquo;s electrical distribution systems serving military and civilian personnel, tenant commands, schools, housing areas, and associated organizations across the installation. Work includes annual preventive maintenance, electrical diagnostics, utility coordination with LUMA, vegetation control, unscheduled service response, and corrective repairs using Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM) or approved parts.</p>

<p>For any request for information on this requirement contact Eileen Rivera at eileen.z.riveradomenech.civ@army.mil and/or Felix Marrero at Felix.r.marrero2.civ@army.mil.</p>
