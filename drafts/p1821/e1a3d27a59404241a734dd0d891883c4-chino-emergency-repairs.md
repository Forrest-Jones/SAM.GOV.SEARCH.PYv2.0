## Description
<p>This project consists of providing all materials, equipment, labor, transportation, and incidentals for completion of the repair storm damage sustained on three buildings at this site.</p>

<p>The repairs are to include roof replacement, dry wall and insulation replacement, a door replacement, ceiling and insulation replacement, installation of gutters and downspouts, and to replace a portion of the side of the Chino Yard Office; to perform maintenance to the metal roofs on the Supervisor&rsquo;s office and the Chino Shop; to repair window frames on upper windows, to caulk ALL of windows, and to install a Z bar at the roof line on the Supervisor&rsquo;s Office; and to gutters and downspouts on the Chino Shop.</p>

<p>This project could also include, if additional funds are available, optional bid items. The optional bid items are exterior painting on the Yard Office; replace the weather stripping on two garage doors and a door sweep on a manway; and if mold is found in the Supervisor&rsquo;s Office to replace the dry wall and insulation.</p>
