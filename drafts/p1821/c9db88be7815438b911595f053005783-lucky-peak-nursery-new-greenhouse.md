<p>This is a combined synopsis/solicitation for commercial items prepared in accordance with the<br />
format in FAR Part 12. This announcement constitutes the only solicitation. Quotations are being<br />
requested and a separate written solicitation will not be issued.</p>

<p><br />
Solicitation number 1240LT26Q0137 is issued as a Request for Quotation (RFQ) for construction of<br />
a new headhouse building, construction of a new Greenhouse building, the post-fire repairs of the<br />
existing greenhouse building, and site support work at the Lucky Peak Nursery site in Boise, Idaho.<br />
An option item for a warehouse building is included. See the SOW for more information.</p>

<p>This acquisition is set-aside for small business concerns. The applicable North American Industry<br />
Classification Standard Code is 236220 The small business size standard is $45 million. This<br />
acquisition is a Total Small Business Set-Aside. All responsible sources may submit a quotation<br />
which will be considered by the agency.</p>
