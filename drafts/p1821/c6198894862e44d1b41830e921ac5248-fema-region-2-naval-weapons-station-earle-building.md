## Description
<p>The Federal Emergency Management Agency (FEMA) hereby issues this Request for Proposal (RFP)<strong> Amendment 0001</strong> for Region 2 Building C-54 at Naval Weapon Station Earle in New Jersey for HVAC Rooftop Units Replacement and Mini Split Units for Server Room &amp; Warehouse to improve system reliability, maintain appropriate indoor environmental conditions, and support continued facility operations.</p>

<p></p>

<p>The contractor shall provide a comprehensive inspection and all labor, equipment, tools, materials, parts, and supplies that are necessary to inspect, remove, replace, repair, install, test, and commission heating, ventilation, and air conditioning systems at Building C-54 at Naval Weapons Station Earle in New Jersey. The work includes replacement of four (4) rooftop HVAC units, removal and installation of two (2) cooling units for the server room, and repair or replacement of two (2) warehouse heating and ventilation units.</p>

<p></p>

<p><strong>Refer to Section L - Instructions, Conditions, and Notices to Bidders for additional updated information. <u>All CLINS need to be proposed to be elegible for award.</u></strong></p>
