The scope of service includes (a) installation of type VS20 film on nineteen (19) rectangular glass panels, two triangle glass panels, and one door; (b) re-paint existing interior painted walls and doors at the Weather Forecast Office (WFO) located in Moon Township, PA; (ii) re-paint the interior of the building.&nbsp; The maintenance work includes: preparing surfaces for the re-coating of primer and/or paint on walls, and metal surfaces.&nbsp; These work areas include offices, rooms, and corridors. Upper Level: Approximately thirteen (13) work areas. The approximate combined wall area is 4,600 sq ft. Approximately ten (10) metal doors with jambs (both sides) on this level will require painting. Additional door jambs with wood doors will require paint on the jambs only. Lower Level: Approximately six (6) work areas. The approximate combined wall area is 2,400 sq ft. Approximately 280 sq ft. will require the removal of carpet wall covering. These areas will involve removal of glue, full spackle, sand and surface preparation to the area, then a final dust wipe down prior to paint. Approximately two (2) metal doors with jambs (both sides) will require painting on this level. Additional door jambs with wood doors will require paint on the jambs only; (iii) Approximately six hundred (600) sq ft of sound barrier wall requires re-habilitation which includes surface preparation, removal / replacement of the top course with new block, and re-painting of both sides of the structure. Approximate wall dimensions: 27 ft overall length,10 ft height.</li>
