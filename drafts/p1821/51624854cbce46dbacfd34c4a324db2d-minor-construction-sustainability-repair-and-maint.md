## Description
<p>The Solicitation will be for projects within the McAlester Army Ammunition Plant (MCAAP). The contractor shall perform all work required as specified within the Statement of Work (SOW) in each individual Task Order (TO). The general description of the work of this Job Order Contract (JOC) includes, but is not limited to, a broad range of sustainability, repair, and maintenance (SRM), and minor construction work on real property at MCAAP. The work is required in support of Directorate of Public Works (DPW) activities.</p>

<p>The contract comprises a one-year (1) base period with four one-year (1) option periods.</p>

<p>This requirement is a total small business set-aside under NAICS 236220 &ndash; Commercial and Institutional Building Construction, size standard $45M.</p>

<p>Individual task orders will be firm-fixed price and contain the following limitations:</p>

<ol>
	<li>
	<ol>
		<li>The Base Year will have an established value with a minimum limit of $5,000 and a maximum limit of $19,800,000.</li>
		<li>Option Periods 1 through 4 will have an established value with a minimum of $5,000 and a maximum limit of $19,800,000.</li>
		<li>The total maximum value of all Task Orders issued under this Master Agreement (including all option years) shall not exceed&nbsp;$99,000,000.00.</li>
	</ol>
	</li>
</ol>

<p>If a required task or material is not listed in the UPB, it shall be classified as a Non-Pre-Priced (NPP) item. The Contractor may then apply their accepted NPP Markup Factor to the lowest responsive quote. NPP items must not exceed 10% of the total cost of any individual Task Order.</p>

<ul>
	<li>Payment &amp; Performance Bonds:&nbsp;The Contractor shall provide a Payment and Performance Bond for 100% of the value of any individual Task Order exceeding $35,000 (or as dictated by local statute).</li>
	<li>Insurance:&nbsp;The Contractor must maintain Commercial General Liability, Worker&rsquo;s Compensation, and Automobile insurance for the duration of this Master Agreement, naming the Owner as an additional insured.</li>
</ul>

<p>If the Contractor fails to complete a Task Order within the time specified in that specific Task Order, the Contractor shall pay MCAAP liquidated damages at the rate specified in the individual Task Order (e.g., $500 per calendar day) until the work is substantially complete.</p>

<p><strong><u>BASIS FOR CONTRACT AWARD</u></strong></p>

<p>(A) Contract Vehicle:<br />
The award will result in a Firm-Fixed-Price (FFP), Job Order Contract (JOC). The awardee will have a total maximum capacity (ceiling) of $99,000,000 over the life expectancy of the contract, which includes a one (1) year base ordering period and four (4) one-year optional ordering periods.</p>

<p>(B) Fair Opportunity:<br />
The Government does not guarantee any specific volume of work beyond the minimum guarantee of $5,000 for the base contract awardee.</p>

<p>(C) Evaluation and Selection:<br />
Award will be made to the responsible offeror whose proposal represents the Best Value to the Government. For the purposes of this solicitation, all non-price factors combined are significantly more important than price. The Government intends to evaluate proposals and award this contract without discussion but reserves the right to conduct discussions if determined necessary.</p>

<p>Proposals will be evaluated based on the following factors:<br />
Factor 1: Past Performance. The Government will evaluate the offeror&#39;s demonstrated record of successfully performing work of similar size, scope, and complexity. Offerors must provide 3-5 completed government projects ranging from $100,000.00 to $3,000,000.00. The evaluation will assess the quality and relevance of the contractor&rsquo;s experience based on the following information for each project:</p>

<ul>
	<li>Contract Number and Value</li>
	<li>Detailed Description of Work</li>
	<li>Demonstrated Technical Quality</li>
	<li>Evidence of Cost Control</li>
	<li>Adherence to Schedule/Timeliness</li>
	<li>Management/Business Relations and Customer Satisfaction</li>
	<li>Small Business Subcontracting achievements</li>
</ul>

<p>Factor 2: Price. The price evaluation will be based on the total evaluated price for the sample TO, derived from the offeror&rsquo;s proposed coefficient. While price is the least important factor, it will be evaluated for fairness and reasonableness.</p>
