## Description
<p>See attachments for full details.&nbsp;</p>

<p>The objective of this project is to extend and install necessary electrical power and plumbing (water supply) from the existing Aerospace Ground Equipment (AGE) Shop to the designated exterior Wash Rack.&nbsp;</p>

f) Factors</p>

<p><br />
1. Technical Acceptability - The offeror must submit a brief technical execution plan (maximum 2 pages) containing a brief execution narrative. Explicitly demonstrate a logical method to safely trench and connect the electrical and plumbing utilities from the existing Shop to the designated Wash Rack based on the requirements mentioned in the Statement of Work (SOW). Demonstrates a clear and realistic schedule showing project completion within the required 100-day Period of Performance (PoP).</p>

<p><br />
2. Past Performance - The Contracting Officer will evaluate past performance using only Government-maintained records and databases. No past performance references, questionnaires, or client lists are required from the offeror. Databases queried will include the Contractor Performance Assessment Reporting System (CPARS), SAM.gov (including FAPIIS / Exclusions), and local Contracting Squadron records in accordance with RFO 12.203(a)(2). The Government reserves the right to utilize past performance information obtained from any other readily available official sources of which it is aware. This factor is rated as &quot;Acceptable&quot; when the queried databases indicate a history of successful contract execution, timely performance, and adherence to safety and code standards. An offeror with no record of relevant past performance, or for whom information on past performance is not available, will receive a rating of &quot;Neutral,&quot; which shall be graded as &quot;Acceptable&quot; and will not bar the offeror from award.</p>

<p><br />
3. Price (Lowest Evaluated Price) - The offeror must submit a single, lump-sum Firm-Fixed-Price (FFP) for CLIN 0001 on their quote sheet (or block 23 of the SF 1449). Price will be evaluated for mathematical accuracy and for price reasonableness in accordance with RFO 12.204(a). Reasonableness will be established by comparing competitive quotes received in response to the solicitation.</p>
