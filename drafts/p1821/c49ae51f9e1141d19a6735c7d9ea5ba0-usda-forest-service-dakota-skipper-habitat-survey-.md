## Description
<p>This is a combined synopsis/solicitation for commercial items prepared in accordance with the format in FAR Part 12 of the Revolutionary FAR Overhaul (RFO).&nbsp; This announcement constitutes the only solicitation. Quotes are being requested and a separate written solicitation will not be issued.&nbsp;</p>

<p>Solicitation number 1284LM27Q0002 is issued as a Request for Quote (RFQ) for Dakota Skipper habitat survey and mapping within&nbsp;the McKenzie Ranger District in McKenzie County, North Dakota on the Little Missouri National Grassland (LMNG).</p>

<p>This acquisition&nbsp;is set-aside for small business concerns. The applicable North American Industry Classification Standard Code is 541620. The small business size standard is $19M. This acquisition is a total small business set-aside. All responsible sources may submit a quotation which will be considered by the agency.&nbsp;</p>

<p>See attachments for project details and submittal instructions. Please direct any questions to joanne.meiers@usda.gov no later than October 28, 2026, COB.&nbsp;<strong>From the date of issuance of this solicitation until a formal contract is awarded and executed, all communication regarding this procurement must be directed exclusively to joanne.meiers@usda.gov. Prospective Contractors, subcontractors, or their representatives are strictly prohibited from contacting any other FS agency personnel prior to award. Any unauthorized contact or attempt to influence agency personnel regarding this active solicitation may result in the immediate disqualification&nbsp;and rejection of the contractor&#39;s quote.</strong></p>
