<p><strong>PROGRAM/PROJECT OVERVIEW: </strong>The 11th Contracting Squadron (11 CONS), District of Washington, has a requirement to execute the design-build upgrades, repairs, and/or replacements to the deteriorated Electrical Switching Stations located at B18 and B168 on Joint Base Anacostia Bolling (JBAB) that includes developing a solution to establish a connection by high voltage electrical redundancy cable. The Government is evaluating the feasibility of executing these requirements via Progressive Design-Build (PDB) utilizing an Other Transaction (OT) Agreement under the statutory authority of 10 U.S.C. &sect; 2808a.&nbsp;</p>

<p>This project summary establishes the operational requirements, technical baseline, and acquisition framework for the Progressive Design-Build (PDB) Other Transaction Authority (OTA) effort to modernize Joint Base Anacostia-Bolling&rsquo;s (JBAB) primary electrical infrastructure.</p>

<p><strong>Project Title</strong></p>

<p>JBAB Electrical Switching Stations (ESS B18 &amp; B168) Modernization &amp; Grid Redundancy</p>

<p><strong>Location</strong></p>

<p>Joint Base Anacostia-Bolling (JBAB), 11th Wing</p>

<p><strong>Acquisition Strategy</strong></p>

<p>Progressive Design-Build (PDB) via Other Transaction Authority (OTA)</p>

<p><strong>Primary Objective</strong></p>

<p>Engineer comprehensive design packages to repair, upgrade, and interconnect deteriorated electrical switching infrastructure</p>

<p><strong>Primary Deliverables</strong></p>

<p>100% executable design packages, specifications, and implementation schedules for ESS upgrades, facility hardening, cable routing, and smart-grid wireless SCADA/telemetry</p>

<p><strong>Scope Breakdown by Facility &amp; Component</strong></p>

<p><strong>ESS B18 (Switching Station Upgrade)</strong></p>

<p>&bull; Full replacement of legacy switchgear with FR3 fluid, air-insulated switchgear (per latest UFC 3-550-01)<br />
&bull; Life-cycle cost and sustainability integration (UFC 1-200-02)</p>

<p>&bull; Thermal partition separating switchgear room from power generation bay<br />
&bull; Dedicated HVAC system and air conditioning ductwork<br />
&bull; Sized space heating units<br />
&bull; High-efficiency interior LED lighting</p>

<p><strong>ESS B168 (Repair, Upgrade &amp; Hardening)</strong></p>

<p>&bull; Complete switchgear replacement mirroring ESS B168b using FR3 air-insulated switchgear (UFC 3-550-01)<br />
&bull; Sustainable design practices (UFC 1-200-02)</p>

<p>&bull; Dedicated single HVAC unit and duct system<br />
&bull; Interior/exterior high-efficiency LED flood/task lighting<br />
&bull; Raised roof profile with comprehensive crack sealing/insulation<br />
&bull; Foundation pillar repairs, crawl space waterproofing, and rainwater gutter system<br />
&bull; Motorized roll-up door, cyber/physical cypher-locked access doors, and motorized loading dock ramp with widened sidewalk</p>

<p><strong>High-Voltage Redundancy Cables</strong></p>

<p>&bull; Design 4-circuit bidirectional interconnect (8 cables total) connecting ESS B18 and ESS B168<br />
&bull; Baseline routing derived from 2025 Engineering Study</p>

<p>&bull; Mandatory underground routing preference across base footprint<br />
&bull; Aerial routing permitted strictly as secondary exception where subsurface obstructions prevent underground installation</p>

<p><strong>Wireless SCADA &amp; Grid Telemetry</strong></p>

<p>&bull; Air Force long-term smart-grid wireless connectivity architecture<br />
&bull; Cell-enabled control panels reporting directly to electric shop web interface without physical comm trunk lines</p>

<p>&bull; Relay-to-control panel comm wiring for ESS B18 and ESS B168<br />
&bull; Additional telemetry control panel integrated into ESS B4505 (sub-station to B18) to monitor sub-station power load profiles</p>

<p><strong>Key Technical Standards &amp; Compliance Baseline</strong></p>

<p><strong>UFC 3-550-01</strong></p>

<p><em>Exterior Electrical Power Distribution</em></p>

<p>Mandates modern FR3 biodegradable, fire-resistant fluid dielectric air-insulated switchgears, significantly mitigating environmental and thermal hazards.</p>

<p><strong>UFC 1-200-02</strong></p>

<p><em>High Performance and Sustainable Building Requirements</em></p>

<p>Governs energy efficiency, life-cycle cost effectiveness, indoor environmental quality, and durable materials integration.</p>

<p><strong>PDB / OTA Authority</strong></p>

<p>Prototype / Collaboration Framework</p>

<p>Fosters early contractor involvement during Phase 1 design, mitigating unforeseen site and subsurface risk prior to Phase 2 construction commitment.</p>
