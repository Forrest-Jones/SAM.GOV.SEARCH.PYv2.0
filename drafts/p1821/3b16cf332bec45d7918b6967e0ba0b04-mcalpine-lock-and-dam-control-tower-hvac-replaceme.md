## Description
<p>PROJECT TITLE: MCALPINE LOCK AND DAM CONTROL TOWER HVAC REPLACEMENT</p>

<p>PROJECT LOCATION: MCALPINE LOCKS AND DAM, 805 NORTH 27TH STREET, LOUISVILLE, KY 40212</p>

<p>THE SCOPE OF WORK INCLUDES THE REMOVAL OF THE EXISTING 3-TON RESIDENTIAL CENTRAL HEAT PUMP SYSTEM, REPLACING IT WITH A NEW 3-TON SYSTEM, REPLACE THE EXISTING THERMOSTAT WITH A NEW PROGRAMMABLE THERMOSTAT, INSTALL NEW THERMOSTAT CONTROL WIRING FROM THE AIR HANDLER/FURNACE TO THE THERMOSTAT LOCATION. ALL WORK SHALL BE PERFORMED IN ACCORDANCE WITH APPLICABLE CODES, STANDARDS, AND MANUFACTURERS RECOMMENDATIONS. THE CONTRACTOR IS RESPONSIBLE FOR PROVIDING ALL LABOR, EQUIPMENT, MATERIALS, AND TOOLS TO COMPLETE THE WORK. PRICES SHOULD INCLUDE MOBILIZATION AND DE-MOBILIZATION, FUEL, LABOR, MATERIAL AND EQUIPMENT.</p>

<p>DISCLOSURE OF THE MAGNITUDE OF THE CONSTRUCTION PROJECT: BETWEEN $25,000 AND $100,000.</p>

<p>THIS PROCUREMENT IS 100% SET-ASIDE FOR SMALL BUSINESS UNDER NAICS 238220, SIZE STANDARD $19,000,000.00.</p>

<p>IN LIEU OF BOTH PERFORMANCE AND PAYMENT BONDS, PER RFO 52.228-13 ALTERNATIVE PAYMENT PROTECTIONS (2000) AND RFO 28.102-1(B), ONLY A PAYMENT BOND OR IRREVOCABLE LETTER OF CREDIT IS REQUIRED.</p>
