## Description
<p>The Tonto National Forest is looking to achieve a more sustainable recreation&nbsp;program. Through this project, several recreation areas will be decommissioned in&nbsp;the Tonto Basin Ranger District (RD), and Globe RD. The decommission of campgrounds&nbsp;at the Tonto Bassin RD is the base bid and at Globe RD, it is an option bid. The&nbsp;intent of this work is to remove and safely dispose outside of the National Forest&nbsp;boundaries, recreational accommodations that include but not limited to Entry&nbsp;station buildings, toilets buildings, sidewalks, asphalt pavement, fish cleaning&nbsp;stations, septic system, and parking lots.</p>
