## Description
<p>This is a performance based, non-personal advisory and assistance services (A&amp;AS) requirement in support of the AFMEDCOM&nbsp; A4/10C programs within the AFMEDCOM in accordance with established program goals and objectives for medical CBRN requirements and execution of medical CBRN defense. The Contractor shall provide a full-range of technical and analytical support for the DAF organizational medical CBRN requirements, readiness, and consequence management to provide installation/homeland and expeditionary/deployed medical response under the umbrella of Air Force Medical All Hazards Support concept. Technical support shall encompass medical CBRN expertise. The Government will retain rights to all intellectual property produced in the course of developing, deploying, training, using, and supporting the AFMEDCOM.</p>

<p>The Government does not intend to award a contract on the basis of the RFI or otherwise pay for the information requested,&nbsp; responses will be treated as information only and not as a proposal.</p>
