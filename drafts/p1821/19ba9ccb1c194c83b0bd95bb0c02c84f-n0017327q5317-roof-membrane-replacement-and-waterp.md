<p>The Government will award a contract resulting from this solicitation to the responsible offeror</p>

<p>whose offer conforming to the solicitation will be most advantageous to the Government, price</p>

<p>and other factors considered. The following factors shall be used to evaluate offers:</p>

<p></p>

<p><strong>Lowest Price Technically Acceptable </strong>- Offers will be ranked lowest to highest according to</p>

<p>price. A price analysis will be conducted to determine whether the lowest price will result from a</p>

<p>single award or multiple awards. Based on the price analysis, the lowest price offer or multiple</p>

<p>offers, will be forwarded to the requiring activity for technical evaluation (offer(s), in accordance</p>

<p>with the specifications, will be deemed either technically acceptable or technically</p>

<p>unacceptable). If the lowest price offer or offers is found technically acceptable and the pricing</p>

<p>determined fair and reasonable by the Contracting Officer, evaluation will be deemed complete</p>

<p>and award will be made based on the lowest price offer(s). If the lowest price offer is determined</p>

<p>technically unacceptable, another analysis will be conducted amongst the remaining offers to</p>

<p>determine if a single or multiple awards will provide the lowest price. The lowest price offer(s)</p>

<p>will be sent for technical evaluation. This process is repeated in order of price until an offer or</p>

<p>combination of offers is deemed technically acceptable and price is determined fair and</p>

<p>reasonable.</p>

<p></p>

<p><strong>Options. </strong>When applicable, the Government will evaluate offers for award purposes by adding</p>

<p>the total price for all options to the total price for the basic requirement. The Government may</p>

<p>determine that an offer is unacceptable if the option prices are significantly unbalanced.</p>

<p>Evaluation of options shall not obligate the Government to exercise the option(s).</p>
