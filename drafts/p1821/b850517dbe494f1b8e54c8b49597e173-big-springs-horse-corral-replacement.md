## Description
<p>An amendment is being publsihed to provide answers to questions and revise SOW-SPECS, this revised document is in the SF30 and documents listed.&nbsp;&nbsp;</p>

<p></p>

<p>This project includes supply and installation of steel oil pipe for one 20&#39;x60&#39; corral with three pens and concrete for post embedment, including temporary traffic control. Local traffic shall be closed during construction with temporary traffic control measures inplace. Including sign stating horse corrals are closed to public for the duration of project.</p>

<p>Optional work that may be awarded includes all work described above associated with:</p>

<p>1.Option #1: Supply and construct one additional 20&#39;x60&#39; corral with three pens.</p>

<p>2.Option #2: Supply and install hitching rail.</p>
