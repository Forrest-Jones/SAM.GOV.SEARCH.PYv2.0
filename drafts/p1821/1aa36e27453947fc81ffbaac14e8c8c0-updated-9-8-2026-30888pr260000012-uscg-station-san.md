## Description
<p><strong>UPDATE: SOLICITATIONS ARE NOW DUE BY MIDNIGHT ON FRIDAY SEPTEMBER 18TH DUE TO COAST GUARD FINANCE MANDATE.</strong></p>

<p><strong>Solicitation: Station San Diego Kitchen Renovation</strong><br />
<strong>Set-Aside:</strong> Total Small Business Set-Aside (FAR Subpart 19.5)<br />
<strong>Location:</strong> USCG Station San Diego, Building F (Kitchen), 2170 N. Harbor Drive, San Diego, CA 92101</p>

<p><strong>Evaluation: FAR&nbsp;52.212-2 - Relevant Past Performance and Price will be used to evaluate bids</strong></p>

<p><strong>1. Project Background and Objective</strong></p>

<p>The U.S. Coast Guard requires complete renovations of the kitchen at Station San Diego (Building F). The Contractor shall provide all personnel, equipment, supplies, transportation, tools, materials, and supervision necessary to modernize the kitchen space.</p>

<p><strong>2. Scope of Work (Summary) -&nbsp;</strong><strong><em>Please review the attached Statement of Work (SOW) for full technical requirements, safety standards, and applicable codes.</em></strong></p>

<p>The Contractor is responsible for the demolition of existing materials and the installation of new cabinetry, countertops, appliances, and fixtures. Key tasks include:</p>

<ul>
	<li>
	<p><strong>Electrical &amp; Plumbing:</strong> Install three (3) new electrical outlets along the SAR Locker Room Wall. Remove the existing garbage disposal and sump pump; furnish and install a new garbage disposal, sump pump, and single-basin stainless steel sink.</p>
	</li>
	<li>
	<p><strong>Cabinetry &amp; Countertops:</strong> Furnish and install new kitchen cabinets and countertops along the Window Wall and SAR Locker Room Wall. Install a new countertop for the Kitchen Island.</p>
	</li>
	<li>
	<p><strong>Appliances:</strong> Furnish and install a new stainless-steel dishwasher, stove, microwave, and a portable stainless-steel refrigerator.</p>
	</li>
	<li>
	<p><strong>Finishing &amp; Painting:</strong> Prepare all wall surfaces (patching, sanding, priming) and paint the entire kitchen space.</p>
	</li>
	<li>
	<p><strong>Testing &amp; Commissioning:</strong> Verify load capacity on new breakers, pressure test plumbing lines, and test all appliance functions.</p>
	</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>3. Site Conditions &amp; Hazardous Materials</strong></p>

<p>An Asbestos and Lead Survey was conducted for this facility (AESS Job No. 26-092).</p>

<ul>
	<li>
	<p><strong>Asbestos:</strong> No asbestos was detected in the Station&#39;s kitchen (Building F).</p>
	</li>
	<li>
	<p><strong>Lead:</strong> Lead was detected at very low concentrations (&lt;0.00151% to &lt;0.00238% by weight) on the east and south walls.</p>
	</li>
	<li>
	<p><strong>Handling &amp; Disposal:</strong> Debris and waste generated from the removal of lead-containing materials must be evaluated for appropriate disposal classification (TCLP or TTLC) prior to off-site disposal. Government dumpsters shall not be used. Contractor shall provide their own dumpster(s).</p>
	</li>
	<li>
	<p><strong>Storage </strong>&ndash; In the past contractors have brought Connex boxes to store their tools and materials. This system has worked out well since Station is very limited on secure storage space.</p>
	</li>
	<li>
	<p><strong>Base Access&nbsp;</strong>- Request contractors get vetted through the Command Security Officer for unrestricted access. A list will be provided to the PM to route to the CSO. If this is not completed contractors will require a Station Member stay on scene with them. Due to the unpredictability of operations, we cannot accommodate that.</p>
	</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>4. Period of Performance and Hours</strong></p>

<p>All work shall be completed within <strong>60 calendar days</strong> from the issuance of the written Notice to Proceed (NTP). Work can be performed 0800 to 1600 Monday through Friday with lunch from 1100 to 1200, excluding federal holidays. Any disruption to utilities must be coordinated at least 72 hours in advance.</p>

<p><strong>5. Wage Determinations</strong></p>

<p>This project is subject to the Davis-Bacon Act (FAR 52.222-6). Prevailing wages for San Diego County, CA, apply. See the attached General Decision Wage Determination.</p>

<p><strong>6. Proposal Submission Requirements</strong></p>

<p>To be considered for award, interested offerors must submit the following documents with their proposal:</p>

<ol>
	<li>
	<p><strong>Product Data Sheets &amp; Warranties:</strong> For all appliances (dishwasher, stove, microwave, refrigerator), sink, garbage disposal, sump pump, cabinetry, and countertops.</p>
	</li>
	<li>
	<p><strong>Samples:</strong> Paint color, countertop, and cabinet finish samples or spec sheets (must be provided for USCG review prior to installation).</p>
	</li>
	<li>
	<p><strong>Safety Plans:</strong> An Activity Hazard Analysis (AHA) &amp; Accident Prevention Plan (APP) detailing Cal/OSHA compliance for lead-containing materials and general construction safety.</p>
	</li>
	<li>
	<p><strong>Technical Plan:</strong> Detailed technical plan outlining the project schedule from the post-award walk-through to construction completion.</p>
	</li>
	<li>
	<p><strong>Past Performance &amp; Licensing:</strong> Relevant past performance examples and proof of valid professional and trade licenses (plumbing, electrical).</p>
	</li>
	<li>
	<p><strong>Pricing:</strong> Transparent pricing sheet breaking down labor, materials, waste disposal, and the total proposed cost.</p>
	</li>
</ol>
