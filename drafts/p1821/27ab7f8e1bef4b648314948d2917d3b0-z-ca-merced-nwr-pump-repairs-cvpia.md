## Description
The U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service has a requirement for deep well repair and rehabilitation at the Merced National Wildlife Refuge in Merced County, California. The work includes diagnostics, well and pump repairs, rehabilitation, electrical work, testing, and commissioning of multiple deep well systems.<br/><br/>Complete requirement details, scope of work, submission instructions, and evaluation criteria can be found within the Combined Synopsis/Solicitation listed below.
