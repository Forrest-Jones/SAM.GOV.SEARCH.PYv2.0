<p><strong>4. Description</strong></p>

<p><strong>(i)</strong> The Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC), on behalf of the National Institute for Occupational Safety and Health (NIOSH), intends to solicit offers for Dose Reconstruction and Related Activities to Support NIOSH&#39;s Responsibilities under the Energy Employees Occupational Illness Compensation Act (EEOICPA). The anticipated contract will support NIOSH&#39;s Division of Compensation Analysis and Support (DCAS) in fulfilling its statutory obligations under EEOICPA by performing radiation dose reconstructions for claims referred by the Department of Labor (DOL) and evaluating petitions for additions of classes to the Special Exposure Cohort (SEC). The anticipated contract type is Cost Plus Award Fee (CPAF). The anticipated period of performance is March 1, 2027 through February 29, 2028 (base year), with four (4) twelve-month option periods through February 28, 2032. Work will be performed at the contractor&#39;s facility.</p>

<p>The scope of work includes the following principal task areas:</p>

<ul>
	<li>Identification of data relevant to reconstructing radiation doses and evaluating SEC petitions</li>
	<li>Claimant communications, including computer assisted telephone interviews</li>
	<li>Dose estimation and reporting</li>
	<li>Special Exposure Cohort petition evaluations</li>
	<li>Technical and program management support</li>
	<li>Operational continuity, cloud migration, and modernization of DCAS systems and processes</li>
</ul>
