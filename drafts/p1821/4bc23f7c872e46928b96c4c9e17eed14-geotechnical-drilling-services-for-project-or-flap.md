## Description
<p>The Western Federal Lands Highway Division (WFLHD) of the Federal Highway Administration (FHWA) is preparing a geotechnical subsurface exploration program for the Winchester Creek Bridge in Douglas County, Oregon. The primary goal is to assess the subsurface conditions for designing a replacement bridge structure.&nbsp; Agency partners include the U.S. Forest Service.</p>

<p>The project is located at latitude N 43.6751&deg; and longitude W 124.1788&deg; in the northeast &frac14; of the southwest &frac14; of Section 7, Township 22 South, Range 12 West of the Bureau of Land Management (BLM) Public Land Survey System (PLSS).</p>
