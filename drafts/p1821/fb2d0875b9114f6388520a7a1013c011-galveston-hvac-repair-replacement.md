<strong>Q33&nbsp;</strong>- Existing Equipment Type &ndash; Turtle Hospital/Wet Lab Observers Area<br />
The SOW identifies four (4) failing systems in the Turtle Hospital/Wet Lab area for replacement with ductless mini-split systems. During the site inspection, we observed that three (3) of the existing units are ductless mini-split systems, while the fourth unit, located in the long room within the Wet Lab/Observers Area, is a conventional split system utilizing existing trunk-line ductwork and supply vents.<br />
Question:<br />
Does the Government require this fourth system to be replaced with a ductless mini-split system as specified in the SOW, or would the Government accept a like-for-like replacement using a conventional split system that utilizes the existing ductwork?<br />

See A14.<br />

<strong>Q39&nbsp;</strong>- Coastal Corrosion Protection<br />
The SOW requires outdoor condensing units to have factory- or field-applied coastal corrosion protection suitable for a marine environment and applied in a manner that does not void the manufacturer&#39;s warranty.<br />
During the site inspection, the on-site POC also confirmed the need for protective coating due to the coastal salt-air environment.<br />
Question:<br />
Does NOAA require a specific manufacturer, product, certification, or coating standard for the coastal corrosion protection, or will any manufacturer-approved marine-grade corrosion protection coating be acceptable?<br />
Additionally, please confirm that this requirement applies to all five (5) outdoor units, including the Wet Lab/Observers Area system if a conventional split system is approved as an alternative.<br />
A39 - No specific manufacturer, product, certification, or coating standard is required. Any factory-applied or manufacturer-approved marine-grade corrosion protection coating suitable for a coastal environment is acceptable, provided it does not void or reduce the manufacturer&#39;s warranty. Coastal corrosion protection is required on both indoor and outdoor units for all five (5) systems, including the Wet Lab Observers Area condensing unit and its matched indoor unit. This expands the Statement of Work requirement, which addressed outdoor condensing units only, and is incorporated by this amendment. See A6.<br />

About preventive maintenance&nbsp;<br />
<strong>Q43</strong>&nbsp;-Preventive Maintenance &ndash; Base Year, Option Years, and Payment: Please confirm whether quarterly preventive maintenance is required during the Base Year or begins with Option Year 1, and whether the preventive maintenance will be paid per service visit or at a fixed annual amount under the applicable CLIN.<br />
A43 - Quarterly preventive maintenance is required during the base year. Per the Statement of Work, preventive maintenance begins following final acceptance of the initial installation and is performed quarterly through the end of the base period, and continues through each successive exercised option year. Each option year includes four (4) quarterly visits as stated in the applicable CLIN.<br />
Because the base period runs one year from date of award and initial installation is not due until 12/31/2026, offerors shall price three (3) quarterly preventive maintenance visits in the base year.<br />
Preventive maintenance shall be priced as a fixed annual amount under the applicable CLIN. Invoicing shall occur in arrears following each completed quarterly visit, in equal installments of that annual amount. All visits shall be coordinated with the Galveston Facility Manager at least five (5) business days in advance and performed during normal business hours unless otherwise approved.<br />
