<p><strong><u>SOLICITATION INFORMATION</u></strong></p>

<p>1. This Request for Proposal (RFP) is being solicited in accordance with the Federal Acquisition&nbsp;Regulations (FAR) Part 19.502-2 Total Small Business Set-Asides and the Partnership Agreement between the Department of Defense and the U.S. Small Business Administration. Competition under the FAR part 6.2 - Full and Open Competition After Exclusion of Sources &ndash; this Requirement has been <strong>100% Set-Aside for Small Business Concerns</strong>.</p>

<p>2. The purpose of the Request for Proposal (RFP) is to procure construction services to <strong>Inspection and Testing of Electric and Diesel Fire Pumps, Engines, Controllers, and Oil/Water Separator at Building R38</strong>, in support of Naval Weapons Station (NWS) Earle. Services are to be performed at Colts Neck, New Jersey.</p>

<p>3. Special clauses and requirements, including wages, work hours, safety and security regulations, etc. for this specific site/location of the work shall be defined herein and be repeated in any resultant contract.</p>

<p>4. Bonding Requirements: None</p>

<p>5. The mandatory Period of Performance: The contractor shall begin performance within 15 calendar days and complete it within <strong>60 calendar days</strong> from date of issuance of Notice to Proceed.</p>

<p>6. Bid shall be valid no less than 60 days after offers are due.</p>
