<p>Solicitation number 127EAX26Q0110 is issued as a Request for Quotation (RFQ) for Greenhouse Refurbishment Project. This project consists of the refurbishment of 4, existing free standing polycarbonate greenhouses maintained by the Center for Medical, Agricultural, and Veterinary Entomology by replacing the infrastructure components and systems to restore greenhouse functionality and provide space to meet USDA-ARS research mission objectives.&nbsp; Located at the Center for Medical, Agricultural, and Veterinary Entomology, Tallahassee, Florida.&nbsp;</p>
