## Description
<p>This is a combined synopsis/solicitation for commercial items prepared in accordance with the format in FAR Part 12.&nbsp; This announcement constitutes the only solicitation.&nbsp; Quotations are being requested and a separate written solicitation will not be issued.&nbsp;</p>

<p>Solicitation number 127EAX26Q0107 is issued as a Request for Quotation (RFQ) for Building 20 Atrium Leak Mitigation</p>

<p>IAW Attachment 1 - Statement of Work:</p>

<p>The intent of this project is to complete inspection and testing of the Building 20 atrium system and provide a report summarizing the source of water leaks that includes photos to document the condition atrium system with recommended repairs that have a cost estimate based on the year of the inspection.&nbsp; The report shall be in order by location and by the urgency of the repair in a prioritized manner.</p>

<p>This acquisition&nbsp; is set-aside for small business concerns.&nbsp; The applicable North American Industry Classification Standard Code is 238160 &ndash; Roofing Contractors&nbsp; The small business size standard is $19M.&nbsp; This acquisition is a Total Small Business Set-Aside.&nbsp; All responsible sources may submit a quotation which will be considered by the agency.</p>
