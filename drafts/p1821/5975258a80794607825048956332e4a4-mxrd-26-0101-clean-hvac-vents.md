## Description
<p>Provide HVAC vent cleaning and related building component replacement services for multiple buildings at Hanscom Air Force Base. The requirement includes cleaning HVAC supply diffusers, replacing 2x2 HVAC return grills, and replacing adjacent 2x2 ceiling tiles in accordance with the quantities and building inventory identified in the Statement of Work. The contractor shall provide all labor, materials, tools, equipment, transportation, supervision, quality control, and project administration necessary to complete the work.</p>
