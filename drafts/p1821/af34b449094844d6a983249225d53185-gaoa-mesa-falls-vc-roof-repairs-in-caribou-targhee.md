## Description
<p>This project consists of roof and other maintenance work on the Mesa Falls Visitor&rsquo;s Center on the Caribou-Targhee National Forest to provide deferred maintenance work. The facility&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; is operated by the Caribou-Targhee NF and Harriman State Park on the Ashton / Island Park Ranger District in Idaho Falls, Idaho.&nbsp; Reroofing, painting / staining the exterior, and other minor repairs will be part of the Base Bid, and snow guards and the repair of all round framing at all overhangs will be Optional Bid Items. The existing corrugated metal panel roofing and all metal trim and flashing will be removed and replaced.&nbsp; All exterior log walls, wood shingle walls, and all trim, doors, and windows will be prepped and stained / painted.&nbsp; Exterior woodpecker damage will be repaired as noted.&nbsp; Damaged overhang framing at one location will be repaired as noted. Work includes the removal and proper disposal of all demolished materials. Contractor shall provide all required materials.</p>
