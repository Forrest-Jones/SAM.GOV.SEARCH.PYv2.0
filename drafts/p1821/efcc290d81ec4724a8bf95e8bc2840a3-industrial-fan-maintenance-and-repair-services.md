## Description
<p>The 316th Contracting Squadron (CONS) has a requirement to obtain preventative maintenance and inspection (PMI) services for approximately twenty-four (24) Big Ass Fans industrial ceiling fans located in aircraft hangars in support of the 89th Maintenance Group (89 MXG) located at Joint Base Andrews MD.</p>

<p>The Contractor shall provide all labor, supervision, tools, and materials to perform annual PMI in accordance with the Performance Work Statement (PWS). A key requirement is that all technicians performing services must hold a current, active &quot;Factory-Certified&quot; or &quot;Factory-Trained&quot; credential directly from the manufacturer (Big Ass Fans) or an authorized partner.</p>

<p>This acquisition is a <strong>100% Total Small Business Set-Aside</strong>. The associated North American Industry Classification System (NAICS) code is <strong>811310</strong> (Commercial and Industrial Machinery and Equipment Repair and Maintenance), with a corresponding small business size standard of <strong>$12.5 million</strong>.</p>

<p>The Government intends to award a single Firm-Fixed-Price (FFP) contract consisting of one (1) 12-month base period and four (4) 12-month option periods. Award will be made on a Lowest Price Technically Acceptable (LPTA) basis.</p>

<p>Interested parties are responsible for monitoring SAM.gov for the full solicitation and any amendments. For full details and instructions, please see the attached documents.</p>
