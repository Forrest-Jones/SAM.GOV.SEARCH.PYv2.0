## Description
<p><strong>This is a SOURCES SOUGHT</strong> notice; a market survey for information only, to be used for preliminary planning purposes.&nbsp; <strong>THIS IS NOT A SOLICITATION FOR PROPOSALS AND NO CONTRACT WILL BE AWARDED FROM THIS NOTICE</strong>.&nbsp; No reimbursement will be made for any costs associated with providing information or industry response to this notice or any follow up information requests.&nbsp; Not responding to this notice does not preclude participation in any future procurement.&nbsp; If a solicitation is released, it will be synopsized in the System for Award Management (SAM).&nbsp; It is the responsibility of potential offerors to monitor SAM for additional information pertaining to this requirement.</p>

<p></p>

<p>The acquisition strategy will be determined based on responses received from this notice and/or other market research methods.&nbsp; The planned acquisition strategy will be posted in SAM upon completion of market research analysis.&nbsp;</p>

<p></p>

<p>The purpose of this notice is to gain knowledge of interest, capabilities and qualifications of industry, to include the Small Business Community: Small Business, Section 8(a), Historically Underutilized Business Zones (HUB-Zone), Service-Disabled Veteran-Owned Small Business (SDVOSB), or Women-Owned Small Business (WOSB). All are highly encouraged to respond.</p>

<p></p>

<p>The U.S. Army Corps of Engineers (USACE), Fort Worth District, intends to solicit and award a contract for site and preparatory construction work associated with an unmanned ILS. The anticipated acquisition will be a competitive, firm-fixed-price (FFP), design-bid-build (DBB), definitive contract. The procurement will be conducted in accordance with RFO 15.103-3, using the Highest Technically Rated with a Fair and Reasonable Price evaluation approach.</p>

<p></p>

<p>The Government is seeking experienced firms to perform site preparation and construction work associated with an unmanned ILS site. The site will accommodate three primary elements of Government-Furnished, Government-Installed (GFGI) electronic equipment under a separate contract: a directional localizer, a glide slope indicator, and radio marker beacons.</p>

<p></p>

<p>The scope of work includes demolition; rerouting existing drainage systems and utilities, as necessary; installation of new utility service lines; gate installation; and site filling and finish grading to support the subsequent installation of the ILS equipment.</p>

<p></p>

<p>The target price range for this project is between $7.8M and $9.5M.</p>

<p></p>

<p>The estimated Period of Performance is 540 calendar days.&nbsp; &nbsp;</p>

<p></p>

<p>The North American Industry Classification System code (NAICS) for this procurement is 238910 &ndash;Site Preparation Contractors, which has a small business size standard of $19.0M.</p>

<p></p>

<p>The Product Service Code is Y1PZ &ndash; Construction of Other Non-Building Facilities</p>

<p></p>

<p>Respondents are reminded that all construction performance must follow the USACE Safety and Health Requirements Manual (EM 385-1-1), and must also comply with all Federal, State and local laws, ordinances, codes and/or regulations.</p>

<p></p>

<p>Anticipated solicitation may be issued within 2nd Quarter of Fiscal Year 2027 with an estimated award between 3rd and 4th Quarter of Fiscal Year 2027.</p>

<p></p>

<p>Small Businesses are reminded that RFO clause 52.219-14 &ndash; Limitations on Subcontracting will be applicable to this contract; and to pay specific attention to paragraphs (e)(3) and (g).</p>

<p></p>

<p>(g) A joint venture agrees that, in the performance of the contract, the applicable percentage specified in paragraph (e) of this clause will be performed by the aggregate of the joint venture participants.</p>
