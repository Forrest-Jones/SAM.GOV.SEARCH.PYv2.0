## Description
<ol>
	<li>This project includes repairing or replacing various HVAC equipment located at various locations across the Payette National Forest.
	<ol>
		<li>The work includes the following:
		<ol>
			<li>McCall Helibase-Replacement of an existing outdoor air conditioning unit</li>
			<li>Krassel District Office-Replacement of existing coil in north outdoor air conditioning unit</li>
			<li>Council Engine Bay-Replacement of an existing ceiling mounted propane unit</li>
		</ol>
		</li>
		<li>Optional work includes the following:
		<ol>
			<li>Supervisors Office-Repair of the three existing systems
			<ol>
				<li>IT room system-Repair leak in outdoor coil and communication issues</li>
				<li>Outdoor air system-Repair identified issues</li>
				<li>Multi-zone system-Repair monitoring of pumps</li>
				<li>Multi-zone system-Install three-phase power monitor &amp; conditioner</li>
			</ol>
			</li>
		</ol>
		</li>
		<li>Quantities associated with these options are identified in the Schedule of Items.</li>
	</ol>
	</li>
</ol>
