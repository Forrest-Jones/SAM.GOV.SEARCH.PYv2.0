## Description
<p><strong>Synopsis:&nbsp;</strong> CONSTRUCTION Indefinite-Delivery Indefinite Quantity (IDIQ) Multiple Award Task Order Contract (MATOC)</p>

<p>The ND Air National Guard, 119th WG, Fargo, ND, is sourcing local (within 250 miles) vendors that are responsible in accordance with Revolutionary Federal Acquisition Regulation Overhaul (RFO) Part 9, a Small Business, and are capable of performing both simple and complex construction services on the ND Air National Guard, 119th Wing.&nbsp; The Government intends to use this information to increase its potential vendor database.</p>

<p>This is a Sources Sought notice and shall not be construed in any manner to be an obligation on behalf of the government, or result in any claim for reimbursement of costs for any effort expanded in responding to this request.</p>

<p>All work will be in accordance with individual task order requirements, specifications and drawings provided with each project or master specifications.&nbsp; Prospective contractors must be able to respond within 4 hours of the 119th Wing Air National Guard, Fargo, North Dakota in response to emergencies via the main office or a staffed satellite office.&nbsp; The government anticipates award of a minimum of two (2) with a maximum of eight (8) individual MATOC contracts, providing sufficient qualified contractors present offers.</p>

<p>These anticipated contracts will consist of a five-year ordering period.&nbsp; Task orders will range between a project magnitude minimum of $2,000.00 to a maximum aggregate value of $25,000,000.00 to any one contractor.&nbsp; All interested&nbsp;contractors must be registered in the System of Award Management (SAM) to be eligible for award.&nbsp; (See internet site https://www.sam.gov/SAM/).&nbsp; Potential contractors should also expect to abide by applicable RFO FAR, Defense Federal Acquisition Regulation Supplement (DFARS), Air Force Federal Acquisition Regulation Supplement (AFFARS), and mandatory procedures.&nbsp; (See internet site https://www.acquisition.gov).&nbsp; Contractors interested in future business with the ND Air National Guard, 119th Wing, must be able to gain access through the appropriate checkpoints and background checks.</p>

<p>The North American Industry Classification System (NAICS) for this acquisition will fall under the following sectors:</p>

<p>22 &ndash; Utilities</p>

<p>23 &ndash; Construction</p>

<p>All interested contractors will provide this office, in writing, with a notice stating their positive intent to submit a proposal as a prime contractor no later than 03 Dec 26.&nbsp; This notification, which shall not exceed five typewritten pages, must include (1) positive statement of intent to bid as a prime contractor, and (2) a completed and signed Sources Sought Information Request Form (see attached) and (3) A listing of projects completed during the past three years, both for government and private industry.&nbsp; The type of project, dollar value, contract number, location and Point of Contact are to be included.&nbsp; Indicate if you were prime of subcontractor, and if subcontractor, provide the name and point of contact for the prime contractor.&nbsp; (4) A brief outline of resources, subcontractors, and key personnel that would be used to accomplish the contract.</p>

<p>In the event Small Business contractors are not available for adequate competition for this project, it may be advertised as unrestricted.&nbsp; Information shall be provided via email to: MSgt Tamara Morris at tamara.morris@us.af.mil, Capt Brian Erdmann at brian.erdmann@us.af.mil and SMSgt Rachel Syverson at rachel.syverson@us.af.mil.&nbsp; Include the following in the subject line &ndash; Sources Sought W50S88-27-MATOC.&nbsp; No facsimile responses will be honored.</p>
