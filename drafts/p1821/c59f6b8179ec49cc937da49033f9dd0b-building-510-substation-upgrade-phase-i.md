<p><br />
Project Description:</p>

<p>The Physics Building was constructed in 1962 and much of the electrical system, original to the&nbsp;building, is beyond its useful life. These systems are also not compliant with current building&nbsp;codes, contain hazardous materials and need complete upgrades. The existing protective devices&nbsp;may not operate as specified, increasing the potential for arc-flash hazards and system damaging&nbsp;electrical faults. Repairs are cost prohibitive and may lead to extended unplanned outages in this&nbsp;mission critical facility.</p>

<p>The Contractor shall provide all labor, supervision, materials (except as noted below), equipment, and services necessary for&nbsp;complete removal and upgrading of the existing&nbsp;Building 510 electrical substation equipment that feeds the newly replaced electrical distribution&nbsp;switchgear. This project includes the removal of two medium voltage substation transformers, one medium&nbsp;voltage oil switch and medium voltage distribution cabling. The project also includes the&nbsp;installation of a new exterior substation with two new medium voltage transformers, one pad&nbsp;mounted medium voltage switchgear and medium voltage feeders in a new concrete encased duct&nbsp;bank. The new substation will be constructed in the north-loading dock parking lot, complete with&nbsp;new transformer and switchgear pads, grounding and crushed stone. Long lead electrical equipment&nbsp;procurements are planned early in the project and construction of the substation infrastructure can&nbsp;be completed in parallel. Once the cutovers are completed the old transformers and oil switch will&nbsp;be removed from the equipment vault in the basement of Building 510. The Contractor shall not procure the switchgear or transformers under this contract.&nbsp;Installation will be executed&nbsp;in a phased approach in order to minimize electric service disruptions to the building and will&nbsp;provide provisions for future redundant circuit configuration.</p>
