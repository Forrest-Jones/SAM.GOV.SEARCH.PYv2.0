## Description
<p>This is a combined synopsis/solicitation for commercial products or commercial services prepared in accordance with RFO FAR Part 12 <em>Acquisition of Commercial Products and Commercial Services</em>. &nbsp;This announcement constitutes this requirement&rsquo;s only solicitation and a separate written solicitation will <strong><u>NOT</u></strong> be issued.&nbsp; &nbsp;&nbsp;</p>

<p></p>

<p>The Regional Contracting Office Hawaii (RCO&mdash;HI) has dining facilities (DFAC) large equipment maintenance and repair services requirement (for single soldiers) on US Army Hawaii installations.&nbsp; This requirement&rsquo;s solicitation number is W912CN-26-Q-A015.&nbsp; The contractor shall provide all resources necessary to complete this requirement on the islands of Oahu and the Big Island (in Hawaii).&nbsp; The solicitation will be a 100% set aside for small business.&nbsp; The government intends to award a single, firm fixed priced contract with one (1) base year and four (4) option years.&nbsp; This solicitation incorporates provisions and clauses by reference.&nbsp; The full text of provisions and clauses may be accessed electronically at&nbsp;www.acquisition.gov.</p>

<p></p>

<p>Award will be conducted under the provisions of Revolutionary FAR Overhaul (RFO) Part 12<em> Acquisition of Commercial Products and Commercial Services</em>, as supplemented by the Defense Federal Acquisition Regulation Supplement (DFARS) and the Army Federal Acquisition Regulation Supplement (AFARS).&nbsp; The prospective quoter must be registered in the System for Award Management (SAM) to be considered for award.&nbsp; It is the prospective quoter&rsquo;s &nbsp;responsibility to monitor this solicitation for the release of amendments (if any) or other information pertaining to the solicitation.&nbsp;</p>

<p></p>

<p>Refer to the attached solicitation, technical exhibits (i.e. equipment lists and forms), and spreadsheet.&nbsp; Prospective vendors must complete the attached solicitation and spreadsheet.&nbsp; Prospective vendors must submit a quote, completed spreadsheet, and all items listed in FAR 52.212-&rsquo;1s Addendum in order to be considered a potential quoter.&nbsp; Any questions can be addressed to Ms. Melena Faxon via E-mail at melena.d.faxon.civ@army.mil.</p>

<p></p>

<p>All completed quotes, Standard Form (SF) 1449 forms, SF 30 forms, spreadsheets, technical capability narrative, resumes, and licenses/certificates must be submitted to Melena Faxon via E-mail at melena.d.faxon.civ@army.mil on November 30, 2026 at 10:00 AM Hawaii Standard Time (HST).&nbsp;</p>
