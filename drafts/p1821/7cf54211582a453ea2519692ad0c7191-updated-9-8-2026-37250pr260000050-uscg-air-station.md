<p><strong>1. Project Background and Objective</strong></p>

<p>The U.S. Coast Guard requires complete renovations of the Aircrew Duty Berthing Room in Building D at Sector San Diego. The Contractor shall provide all personnel, equipment, supplies, transportation, tools, materials, and supervision necessary to modernize the space for active-duty personnel.</p>

<p><strong>2. Scope of Work (Summary) -&nbsp;<em>Please review the attached Statement of Work (SOW) for full technical requirements, safety standards, and applicable codes.</em></strong></p>

<p>The Contractor is responsible for the demolition of existing materials and the installation of new commercial-grade fixtures. Key tasks include:</p>

<ul>
	<li>
	<p><strong>Lighting Renovation:</strong> Replace existing light fixtures with new fully dimmable LED fixtures featuring a selectable red-light mode across 6 sleeping rooms, 1 central hallway, and 1 bathroom.</p>
	</li>
	<li>
	<p><strong>Bathroom Renovation:</strong> Demolish existing fixtures, tile, and shower pan. Install a new commercial-grade toilet, sink/vanity with enclosed storage, shower pan, low-maintenance sheet vinyl flooring, and a new mirror.</p>
	</li>
	<li>
	<p><strong>Flooring Installation:</strong> Remove approximately 590 sq ft of existing carpet and install new Luxury Vinyl Plank (LVP) flooring, including new baseboards and trim.</p>
	</li>
	<li>
	<p><strong>Interior Painting:</strong> Prepare and paint all interior walls and trim in the 6 sleeping rooms and the common passageway.</p>
	</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>3. Site Conditions &amp; Hazardous Materials</strong></p>

<p>An Asbestos and Lead Survey was conducted for this facility (AESS Job No. 26-092).</p>

<ul>
	<li>
	<p><strong>Asbestos:</strong> No asbestos was detected in the Berthing Room in Building D.</p>
	</li>
	<li>
	<p><strong>Lead:</strong> Lead was detected at very low concentrations (&lt;0.00146% to 0.0039% by weight). While below the EPA/HUD threshold for Lead-Based Paint, <strong>any detectable level of lead triggers Cal/OSHA Lead in Construction Standard (8 CCR &sect; 1532.1) compliance.</strong></p>
	</li>
	<li>
	<p><strong>Handling &amp; Disposal:</strong> Debris and waste generated from the removal of lead-containing materials must be evaluated for appropriate disposal classification (TCLP or TTLC) prior to off-site disposal. Government dumpsters shall not be used.</p>
	</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>4. Period of Performance and Hours</strong></p>

<p>All work shall be completed within <strong>60 calendar days</strong> from the issuance of the written Notice to Proceed (NTP). Work hours are Monday through Friday, 0800 to 1700 hours, excluding federal holidays. Any disruption to utilities must be coordinated at least 72 hours in advance.</p>
