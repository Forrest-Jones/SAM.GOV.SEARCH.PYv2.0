<p>The United States Army Corps of Engineers (USACE) Seattle District anticipates solicitation and award of a Firm Fixed-Price contract resulting from Request for Proposal (RFP) W912DW27RA017. This JBLM MDTF BLDGs 3221-3222 acquisition will utilize a design that will convert facilities, in the 3200 block of Lewis Main, for the purposes of supporting operational space for the MDTF.</p>

<p></p>

<p>Construction services will support the renovation and conversion of two separate and existing 1950&rsquo;s era Barracks Facilities comprising approximately 63,400 SF of combined renovation work. The two facilities are approximately the same size. Building 3221 is a former 165-personnel &ldquo;hammerhead&rdquo; barracks facility which is to be converted to indirect fire protection capability (IFPC) with a combined COF/Battalion HQ consisting of a first-floor Battalion HQ with company operations space on the second and third floors. Building 3222 is a former 165-personnel &ldquo;hammerhead&rdquo; barracks facility which is to be converted into a combined administration, readiness, and troop aid facility.</p>
