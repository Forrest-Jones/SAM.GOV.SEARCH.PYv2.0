## Description

<br />
DESCRIPTION:</strong> The U.S. Army Corps of Engineers (USACE) Louisville District intends to issue an Invitation for Bid (IFB), Solicitation No. W912QR27BA001, for the repair of building 1203 located at March Air Reserve Base, CA. A description of the construction project is as follows:</p>

<p></p>

<p>This project will address the mechanical system deficiencies and the sprinkled water supply line into Building 1203 (34,281 SF) at March Air Reserve Base, CA. The scope of work shall include replacement of the entire mechanical systems, including ductwork, sensors, air handling units 1 and 2, and associated controls including fire alarm controls. In addition, the project scope will include replacement of the existing main fire water supply line from the street main to the riser room. The PIV (post indicator valve) shall be replaced as part of the project scope. Testing and balancing, and commissioning, of the complete mechanical system and fire suppression systems shall be completed following the installation work.</p>
