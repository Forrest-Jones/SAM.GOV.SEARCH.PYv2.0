## Description
<p>The Norman A. Berg Plant Materials Center facilities need repairs and upgrades. The center requires a newelectrical hookup for a lab trailer and electrical upgrades for the two greenhouses (indicated as North Greenhouse and South Greenhouse) and the upper garage. Both greenhouses require foundational repairs and re-parging. One greenhouse requires a commercial grade greenhouse door replacement.</p>

<p><u><strong>SITE VISIT</strong></u>: Interested vendors can review the work site by attending the Site Visit on August 25, 2026, at 10:00am.<br />
<u><strong>Site Visit Location:</strong></u> 8791 Beaver Dam Road, Beltsville MD 20705<br />
<u><strong>Site Visit POC:</strong></u> Dan Dusty; Farm Manager at dan.dusty@usda.gov, 301-289-9776.</p>

<p>Responses are due by 2:00 p.m. ET on September 16, 2026 by email to Bobby Copenny, II, Contract Specialist, at bobby.copenny@usda.gov.<br />
This is a request for quote (RFQ) solicited using the simplified acquisition procedures of FAR Part 13.</p>

<p><br />
Contractors are strongly urged to attend the site showing.<br />

at outlined in statement of work and detailed in the specifications, drawings, and plans attached hereto.</p>

<p><br />
