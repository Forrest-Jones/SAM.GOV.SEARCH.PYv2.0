## Description
<p>This requirement is for two air compressor relocations for US Naval Research Laboratory (NRL). The full description and response requirements are included in the Request for Quotes (RFQ) N0017326Q1905 along with attachments. &nbsp;Your quick response is appreciated to facilitate an award prior to current fiscal year end.</p>

<p>A site visit is required before submitting any proposal or quote. The&nbsp;<strong>mandatory site visit is scheduled for Wednesday, October 7, at 9:30 am EDT at NRL</strong>. If you are unable to attend on this date, another date may be requested, although the requested date/time may not be available.&nbsp; No change to the response date and time specified in Block 8 of the Standard Form (SF) 1449 will be granted based on an inability to visit NRL on the planned site visit day.
