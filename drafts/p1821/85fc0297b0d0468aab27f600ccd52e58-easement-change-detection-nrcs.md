## Description
<p>The Contractor shall design, maintain, and operate an automated geospatial data processing system that supports nationwide monitoring of all NRCS conservation easements. The system shall analyze satellite, aerial, and other remotely sensed datasets; perform preprocessing; and ensure alignment with NRCS quality standards.</p>
