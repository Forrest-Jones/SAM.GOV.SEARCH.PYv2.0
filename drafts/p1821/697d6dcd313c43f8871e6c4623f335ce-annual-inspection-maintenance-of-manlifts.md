## Description
<p>The U.S. Coast Guard Academy in New London, Connecticut, intends to solicit for preventive maintenance, annual inspection, and corrective maintenance services for Government-owned mobile elevating work platforms (MEWPs), including scissor lifts and boom lifts. The Contractor shall furnish qualified competent personnel, labor, supervision, tools, test equipment, transportation, consumables, and quality control necessary to inspect and maintain the equipment in accordance with applicable U.S. Coast Guard Maintenance Procedure Cards, OSHA requirements, ANSI/SAIA A92 standards, original equipment manufacturer specifications, and all other applicable federal, state, and local requirements. Required services include firm-fixed-price annual preventive inspections for scissor lifts and boom lifts, with corrective maintenance, troubleshooting, repair, replacement, and adjustment services to be ordered on a time-and-materials basis through task orders upon Government approval.</p>
