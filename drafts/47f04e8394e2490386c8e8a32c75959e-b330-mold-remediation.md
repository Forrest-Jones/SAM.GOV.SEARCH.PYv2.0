<p>2. Scope of Work &amp; Line Items</p>

<p>2.1 Description of Requirement</p>

<p>The work described herein includes the furnishing of all materials, equipment, and labor to perform the work as described in the attached Statement of Work (SOW).</p>

<p>The purpose of this project is to provide professional mold remediation services in a 485 SF Office (B330 Break RM) below and above the ceiling grid, and monitoring for an additional adjacent 580 SF office space (&ldquo;LRS CC RM&rdquo;) in Building 330 at Joint Base Langley&ndash;Eustis, JBLE&ndash;Langley AFB, Hampton, VA. The scope includes negative pressure containment, decontamination, cleaning, sanitizing, and disposal of contaminated cabinetry, wall boards, and ceiling tiles, with an option for the installation of new mold-tough drywall sheetrock, rock wool sound insulation, and USG ceiling tiles.</p>

<p>All work shall conform to industry standards. Any deviation from the Manufacturer installation requirements must be approved prior to performing the work on site.</p>
