## 5. Technical approach (Pass / Fail)

The RFQ rates technical approach Pass or Fail on one thing: whether the quote "clearly and fully" demonstrates understanding of the SOW and **similar and relevant experience of construction repair/replacement of standing seam metal roofing.** Section 5.5 is where that is decided.

### 5.1 Understanding of the requirement

**[DRAFT — your review]** Eight buildings on the USDA-ARS Tifton campus receive new 26-gauge (field-verified) standing seam metal roofs, hunter or forest green, matching the existing standing seam roofs on campus, with matching gutters, leaf guards and downspouts where the SOW calls for them. Existing metal roofing is removed and disposed of off-site, roofs are stripped to underlayment, decking is inspected before any new material goes on, and every penetration is flashed and sealed. All work is turn-key, on an active research campus, within 60 calendar days of Notice to Proceed, between 7:00 am and 4:00 pm Monday to Friday.

### 5.2 Means and methods, in SOW order

**[DRAFT — your review]**

- **Submittals before ordering (SOW 1.2, 2.1, 10).** Within [[FILL: days]] of award we submit the standing seam profile for lab approval as the closest match to the existing campus roofs, and a manufacturer color chart for the hunter/forest green selection. No material is ordered until both are approved in writing.
- **Removal and decking inspection (SOW 2.1).** Each roof is stripped to underlayment and the decking inspected for integrity and leaks before installation. Defective decking is photographed and reported to the CO and COR as a differing site condition under FAR 52.236-2; no decking replacement is priced in this quote, per SOW 2.1.
- **Installation.** 26-gauge standing seam panels installed to the manufacturer's published details and to local, state and federal code, with particular attention to wind-loading requirements (SOW 2.1). Metal flashing and sealing at every penetration: the fume hood, chimney and four drain vents at the Head House; two drain vents at the Insectary. Gutters with leaf guards and downspouts to match the roofing at Options 1, 2, 4, 5, 6 and 7. The awning joining the greenhouse at Option 2 is removed and replaced.
- **Sequencing on an active campus (SOW 4.3, 4.5).** Building-by-building, coordinated with the COR so no removal disturbs a system that supports ongoing research. Work area barricaded; dust and noise abatement continuous; nothing left open overnight.
- **Disposal (SOW 7).** Removed metal recycled to the maximum extent practicable; all other debris hauled off-site at frequent intervals and disposed of legally. No burning, no burying. Any suspect material stopped and reported to the CO/COR.
- **Site logistics (SOW 2.2, 8).** Own restroom facility; no Government equipment used; utilities requested from the Government only if needed; any shutdown requested three working days ahead.
- **Cleanup and inspection (SOW 9, 11).** Site clean daily; inspection scheduled in writing to the COR and CO at least three days ahead of each building's completion.
